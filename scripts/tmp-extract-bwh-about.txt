About BWH Hotels — official enterprise overview (bestwestern.com corporate about page; BWH Hotel Group).

BWH Hotels is a leading global hospitality enterprise comprised of three hotel companies including WorldHotels, Best Western Hotels & Resorts and SureStay Hotels. The global enterprise boasts approximately 4,300 hotels in over 100 countries and territories worldwide.

With 18 unique brands across every chain scale segment, from economy to luxury, BWH Hotels suits the needs of developers and guests in every market as each brand offers its own personality and style. At the heart of each of these brands is the caring spirit shared by hoteliers and their hotel teams across the globe. They are united by their deep commitment to delivering exceptional experiences and exceeding guest expectations with every stay.

Vision: Inspiring travel through unique experiences.

Mission: Deliver trusted guest experiences, drive hotel success, and foster a caring inclusive culture that respects the environment.

Hotel Development: BWH Hotels is driving innovation and growth across hotel categories with experiences that cater to modern traveler and developer needs.
