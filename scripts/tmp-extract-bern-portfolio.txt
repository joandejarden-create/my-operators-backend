Hoteles Bern da la bienvenida a los visitantes y turistas — Empresas Bern (official parent company blog, October 27, 2025).

Bern Hotels & Resorts Panama and adventure travel company Panamazing reopened properties welcoming visitors to Panama following COVID-19 closures, operating within CDC and government guidelines.

Properties referenced (with official third-party brand links as cited by Bern corporate):

- The Westin Playa Bonita Panama — https://www.marriott.com/hotels/travel/ptybw-the-westin-playa-bonita-panama/
- Crowne Plaza Panama Airport — https://www.ihg.com/crowneplaza/hotels/us/en/panama/pcyap/hoteldetail/hotel-overview
- InterContinental Miramar Panama — https://www.ihg.com/intercontinental/hotels/us/en/panama-city/pcyha/hoteldetail/concierge
- Dreams Playa Bonita (reopening noted April 2021 at time of article) — https://www.dreamsresorts.com/en_us/resorts/panama/playa-bonita/photos-videos.html
- The Westin Panama — https://www.marriott.com/hotels/travel/ptywi-the-westin-panama/

Quote — Herman Bern, Jr., President, Bern Hotels & Resorts Panama: "Estamos muy emocionados de poder dar la bienvenida a los visitantes a nuestros hoteles."

Operational notes from article: on-property antigen testing for guests staying five+ nights; group events up to 25 people (special health ministry permits for larger events through Bern hotels).

Source: Empresas Bern corporate site (real-estate parent of Bern Hotels & Resorts Panama). Region: Panama / Central America (not Dominican Republic).
