## “The Radisson Difference” commercial / owner bullets

- **Brand awareness** — Long heritage; **Choice Privileges** called out as the loyalty engine in this Choice-affiliated positioning; recognition drives occupancy/revenue narrative.
- **Designed for scalability** — Airports, convention centers, resorts; varied F&B and amenity needs.
- **Conversion made easy** — Prototype for varying layouts, room sizes, HVAC, bathrooms; **kit-of-parts**; case goods for install; built-in electrics; retain viable elements (e.g. vanities) for speed and cost.
- **Operational efficiency** — Efficient labor model; **minimal F&B requirements**; guest room designed for **ease of maintenance and cleaning**; profitability + guest appeal framing.
