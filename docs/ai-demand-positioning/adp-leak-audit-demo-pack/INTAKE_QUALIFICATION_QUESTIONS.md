# Internal Qualification Questions — AI Demand Leak Audit

Required intake questions before approving / running a free audit.

Use these on the admin intake form notes, LinkedIn reply thread, or a short intake call.

## Required 5

1. **Hotel name and website**  
   Exact property name + primary website URL.

2. **Your role with the hotel**  
   Owner, asset manager, GM, operator, brand, advisor, or other.

3. **Market / location**  
   City / market / country as the prospect understands them.

4. **Which demand segment matters most right now?**  
   Examples: leisure, couples / romantic, meetings / groups, wellness, family, business travel, celebration / weddings.

5. **Are you open to a paid monthly pilot if the audit identifies meaningful gaps?**  
   Yes / maybe / not now.  
   Purpose: protect free-audit capacity for prospects who can convert to paid ADP conversation.

## Soft pass / fail guidance

| Signal | Guidance |
|--------|----------|
| Clear hotel + decision role + open to pilot | Strong approve |
| Curious but not decision-maker | Approve only with champion path; set expectations |
| Wants free forever / full ADP for free | Decline or redirect to paid pilot discussion |
| No website / unclear property identity | Pause until identity is clear |

## Mapping to admin form

| Question | Form field |
|----------|------------|
| Hotel name and website | `hotelName`, `hotelWebsite` |
| Role | `role` |
| Market / location | `city`, `market`, `country` |
| Demand segment | `demandSegmentOfInterest` |
| Open to paid pilot | Capture in `notes` until a dedicated field is added in a later phase |
