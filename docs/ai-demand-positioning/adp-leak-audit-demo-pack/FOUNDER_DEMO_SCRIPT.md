## 5-minute deeper explanation

### What the audit does
Runs a **limited** set of demand scenarios across a small provider sample for one hotel. It is designed to reveal mention gaps and competitor displacement — not to replace full ADP.

### What a demand leak is
A demand leak is when AI answers for a valuable traveler intent (meetings, couples, leisure, etc.) name competitors while the subject hotel is absent or weakly represented.

### How competitor displacement works
For each monitored answer we check: was the subject hotel mentioned? If not, which competitors were? Displacement count is how often that pattern appears in the limited sample.

### Why the report is limited
- Controlled sample size
- Not monthly longitudinal monitoring
- Not the full ADP prompt universe
- AI answers can vary by provider, timing, and framing

### What Dealality can prepare with AI
Source audits, gap analysis, draft copy, competitor comparison, checklists, evidence packs.

### What the hotel must approve / publish
Facts, claims, final language, publishing decisions, and the actual website / OTA / GBP / review updates.

### How this leads into paid ADP
The free audit creates shared evidence. The paid pilot measures change over time and supports execution — without turning the free audit into a full ADP giveaway.

## Objection answers

| Objection | Answer |
|-----------|--------|
| “Can’t I just ask ChatGPT myself?” | You can, but answers vary and you will not get a structured leak view, competitor displacement counts, or an action split between Dealality prep vs hotel publish. |
| “Is this the full ADP product?” | No. This is a limited diagnostic. ADP is monthly monitoring, deeper review, playbooks, and review meetings. |
| “Will fixing these three items guarantee better AI rankings?” | No. We do not promise ranking or booking lift. We help strengthen public signals and then monitor whether visibility changes. |
| “Do you change our website for us?” | We prepare drafts and checklists. The hotel or its agency approves and publishes. |
| “Why should we pay after a free audit?” | Because one snapshot is not enough. Paid ADP tracks whether the hotel is still missing, whether competitors keep displacing it, and whether actions are working over time. |
| “Our brand / operator already has visibility work.” | This is complementary. It focuses on AI answer displacement for specific demand segments, not generic SEO alone. |
