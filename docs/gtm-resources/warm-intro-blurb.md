# Warm Intro Blurb (Copy/Paste Ready)

**Status:** Final — Jul 2026  
**Use:** Lawyers, advisors, consultants, brokers, and referral sources forward this to an owner, investor, or operator contact **with that contact's opt-in**.  
**Related:** Pilot overview one-pagers (Owner/Developer, Lawyer/Advisor Referral, Advisor/Consultant)

---

## When to use

- A contact agrees to make an owner-opt-in introduction.
- You want a **short, forwardable paragraph** they can paste into email or LinkedIn.
- You are **not** asking them to share confidential client pipelines.

## Standard forward (email)

**Subject (introducer writes):** Quick intro — Joan / Dealality

```
Hi {{recipient_first_name}},

I wanted to connect you with {{your_name}}, who is testing a small hospitality pilot called Dealality.

{{your_name}} is working with a limited group of owners and advisors to help structure hotel opportunities before brand/operator conversations — assessing readiness, comparing options, and clarifying what information may still be missing. {{owner_context}}

This is early and intentionally small. There is no obligation, and any real opportunity would move forward only with the owner's opt-in.

I'll let you two take it from here if useful.

Best,
{{introducer_first_name}}
```

## Joan reply (after double opt-in)

Use when the introducer copies you in and the recipient is open to a conversation:

```
Hi {{recipient_first_name}},

Thank you, {{introducer_first_name}}, for the introduction — and {{recipient_first_name}}, nice to meet you.

I'm testing a small Dealality pilot with owners and advisors in CALA and adjacent markets. The goal is practical: take one real or realistic opportunity, assess readiness, compare brand/operator alignment options, and clarify what may still be missing before conversations move too far.

I'm keeping the first group small. If helpful, I'd welcome a short conversation to see whether this might be relevant — whether you have an active opportunity, a realistic scenario, or simply a useful perspective.

Would a 20-minute call sometime in the next week or two work? I'm flexible on timing.

At your service,
Joan
```

## Tone guardrails

**Do**

- Keep it low-pressure and owner-opt-in.
- Frame as a **small pilot** — learning, not a product launch pitch.
- Emphasize structure, readiness, and comparison **before** brand/operator outreach.
- Thank the introducer; make it easy for them to step back.

**Do not**

- Promise outcomes, matches, funding, or brand introductions.
- Imply Dealality is a listing service, broker, or operator.
- Ask the introducer to share confidential client details without owner consent.
- Use defensive language ("not a mass outreach," "not a public listing," etc.).
