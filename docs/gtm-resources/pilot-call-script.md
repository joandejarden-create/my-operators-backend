## Opening (all segments)

> Thanks for making time — I really appreciate it.
>
> I'm testing a small Dealality pilot with a limited group of owners and advisors. The goal today is simple: understand whether this might be useful for you — whether that's an active opportunity, a realistic scenario, feedback on the workflow, or a future introduction.
>
> This is early-stage and low-pressure. Nothing moves forward without the owner's opt-in, and we can keep things high-level until everyone is comfortable.
>
> Does that framing work for you?

---

## Lawyer / advisor branch

**Set context:**

> You often see deals before they're fully structured for brand or operator conversations. Dealality is meant to help owners and advisors assess readiness, compare alignment options, and see what may still be missing — before outreach goes too far.

**Ask:**

1. Are you advising an owner on a hotel opportunity today — or mainly exploring whether this kind of structure would be useful in your practice?
2. If there is a client situation, is the owner open to a structured comparison workflow (with their opt-in)?
3. What usually goes wrong or gets expensive when owners approach brands/operators too early?
4. Would a short pilot on one real or realistic scenario be useful — or is feedback on the concept enough for now?

**If referral only:** offer warm intro blurb; do not push platform access.

---

## Consultant / broker branch

**Set context:**

> You sit close to owner conversations about positioning, timing, and who to approach. The pilot is about structuring and comparing options before those conversations — not replacing your role or intermediating relationships.

**Ask:**

1. Do you have an owner or project in mind where better readiness or comparison would help — or is this more about whether the workflow fits how you advise?
2. How do you usually help owners prepare before brand/operator outreach?
3. What information do owners typically lack at that stage?
4. If relevant, would the owner be open to exploring this with their opt-in — or would you prefer to stay at feedback/referral for now?

**Clarify if needed:** Dealality is not a brokerage; you remain in control of client relationships.

---

## Owner / developer branch

**Set context:**

> Dealality helps owners structure one opportunity at a time — assess readiness, compare brand/operator alignment, and clarify gaps before conversations move too far. You stay in control of what is shared and what moves forward.

**Ask:**

1. Is there a specific project or scenario you'd want to work through — location, asset type, stage?
2. What are you trying to decide in the next 6–12 months (reflag, operator, brand, restructuring, new build)?
3. What do you already know vs. what still feels unclear before talking to brands or operators?
4. Who else is involved (advisor, lawyer, capital partner) and would they need to be part of a pilot?

**If early-stage:** realistic hypothetical is OK for feedback; real pilot needs defined subject + opt-in.

## Core discovery questions (any segment)

Use as needed; don't read as a checklist.

| Topic | Question |
|-------|----------|
| Subject | Is there one primary opportunity or scenario, or mainly general perspective? |
| Timing | Any decision, LOI, RFP, or brand/operator conversation on the horizon? |
| Objective | Reflag, new build, operator search, brand comparison, or restructuring? |
| Geography | Where is the project (city/country)? CALA vs other? |
| Information | What would you be comfortable sharing at a high level today? |
| Success | What would make this call useful for you in 30 minutes? |

## Feedback vs real opportunity (decision on call)

Ask explicitly (from acceptance criteria):

1. *"Is there a specific opportunity or realistic scenario you'd want to structure — or is this mainly perspective / referral?"*
2. *"If we proceed, anything shared moves forward only with the owner's opt-in — does that work?"*
3. *"What would make this a useful use of our time?"*

| Signal on call | Path |
|----------------|------|
| Owner opt-in + defined subject + scope fits | **Real pilot opportunity** → note gaps; schedule follow-up for intake |
| Useful perspective, no active deal | **Feedback / referral only** → thank, optional one-pager, warm intro if offered |
| Wrong timing or fit | **Follow-up later** or **not a fit** → set PTL accordingly |

---

## Confidentiality & opt-in (scripted)

> Just to be clear: I'm not asking you to share confidential materials on this call unless you're comfortable. If we explore a real opportunity later, we agree on what's shared, who sees it, and whether you want to use the platform for structured comparison — or keep it conversational. Nothing goes to brands or operators without your control.

---

## Close & next steps

**Real pilot candidate:**

> This sounds like it could be a good fit for the pilot. Next step would be a short follow-up to capture the basics we discussed and see if structured comparison would help — still with your opt-in. I'll send a brief summary and we can pick a time.

**Feedback / referral only:**

> This was really helpful — thank you. I'll follow up with the one-pager if useful, and if you think of an owner who'd welcome a conversation, I can send a short intro blurb you can forward.

**All calls:**

> Thanks again for your time. I'll log a few notes on my side and follow up with one clear next step.

## Tone guardrails

- Listen more than pitch; one clear next step at close.
- No promises on brand introductions, funding, or guaranteed matches.
- Do not demo live platform unless opportunity is accepted and QA complete.
- Match reply playbook tone: personal, concise, low-pressure.
