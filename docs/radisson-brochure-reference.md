## Guest & target insight

### Target insight (verbatim theme)

Hotels today feel **impersonal and standardized**; guests appreciate modern efficiency but **long for authenticity and genuineness** of a **bygone era of travel**.

### Meet the Authentic Journeymen

- **Who:** Individuals and groups **25–45**, frequent **business travelers**, also leisure/social contexts.
- **What they seek:** **Simplicity and authenticity**; **genuine connection** and **human touch**; **modern imperatives** (tech, convenience); **personal growth** and experiences that **matter**.

Use for **`overview.owner_experience`**, **`hero.operator_compat`**, or audience lines—align with **Basics** guest fields where you store psychographics.

## Key experiences (four quadrants)

1. **Functional guest rooms**
2. **Flexible food & beverage model**
3. **Meeting space**
4. **Fitness & wellbeing**

### Functional guest rooms — three proof blocks

| Theme | Idea |
|-------|------|
| **Cozy nook** | Signature seating corner—comfort, respite from the day; TV, reading, drink. |
| **Ample storage** | Innovative **bed + wardrobe**; blend **open and concealed** storage. |
| **Touches that matter** | Well-lit **workspace**, great shower, **quality essentials**. |

### Flexible F&B — three concepts

| Concept | Role |
|---------|------|
| **The coffee counter** | Integrated with **check-in or bar**; barista-style coffee, cold brew, pastries; morning or afternoon break. |
| **The marketplace** | Near reception; **grab-and-go** all day—sandwiches, salads, snacks, drinks. |
| **The dinette & bar** | **À la carte** breakfast and dinner; **American classics**; bar with **retro cocktails** (e.g. Vesper, Old Fashioned)—nostalgic yet sophisticated. |

### Meeting space

Comfortable, convenient venue for **corporate**, **sporting**, **family** gatherings; small memorable touches (e.g. **nostalgia-inspired milkshake break**).

### Fitness & wellbeing

**24/7** fitness centers; **cardio** and **weights**; dedicated **yoga** and **stretching** zones—convenience for **mind and body** while traveling.
