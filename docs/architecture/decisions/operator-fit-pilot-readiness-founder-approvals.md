# ADR — Operator Fit Pilot Readiness Founder Approvals

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Phase:** Pilot Readiness Gate (no My Deals wiring)

---

## 1.1 Wave 2 persistence

Approve persistence of reviewed Wave 2 research for Highgate, Atlantica, Driftwood, and Santa Fe where source / publication / conflict / taxonomy / evidence policies pass.

**Do not persist into owner-facing fields:** Internal Only; unsupported performance; project-specific availability/fees/contract terms/proposed teams; unverified brand approvals; Strategic Interest as operating presence; sensitive negatives without review.

## 1.2 Market Presence Type

Approve minimum persistent Market Presence architecture distinguishing:

Current Managed Property · Current Operating Portfolio · Regional Office or Team · Active Development · Historical Presence · Strategic Interest · Claimed Capability · Unknown.

Country membership alone is **not** sufficient for Operator Fit geographic eligibility. Strategic Interest must never score equivalently to current operating presence.

## 1.3 Cenote remediation

`Active Countries` must stay consistent with Market Presence. Do not restore rejected unsupported countries. Non-operating facts (Claimed Capability / Strategic Interest / Historical) live only as Market Presence rows.

## 1.4 Deal B

Do **not** weaken geography eligibility or readiness thresholds. Prefer zero Ranking Ready over false candidates. Solve via evidence, presence modeling, Argentina-capable research, and universe expansion only when justified.

## 1.5 Shortlist architecture

Design a dedicated Operator Shortlist conceptually distinct from Target List, Operator Deal Request, Outreach, and Contacted. **No production persistence in this phase.**

## 1.6 Pilot flag model

`OPERATOR_FIT_ENGINE_V2` remains globally default **off**. Future pilot must be server-controlled (internal/admin, allowlisted users/deals, environment). No client-side-only enablement. No owner enablement in this assignment.

## Explicit non-goals

My Deals wiring · owner access · production Shortlist records · pathway matrix · OAS deprecation · owner weights · outreach automation.
