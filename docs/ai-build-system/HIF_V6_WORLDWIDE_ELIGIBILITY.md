# HIF V6 — Worldwide Eligibility (Permanent Founder Directive)

**Effective:** 2026-11-21  
**Status:** Binding for all HIF tracks  
**Module:** `lib/hotel-intelligence/sec-edgar/hif-v6-worldwide-eligibility-v6.js`

## Principle

HIF is a **worldwide** hotel development opportunity discovery platform.

The United States, Mexico, Brazil, United Kingdom and Canada are the **initial five priority markets** for building commercial critical mass.

They are **not** geographic restrictions on research, discovery, qualification, storage or future expansion.

## Required behavior

1. Search globally across hotel, securities, contractual, corporate, regulatory and commercial-event sources.
2. Accept and validate opportunities in **every** country under existing evidence standards.
3. Research international issuers even when filing jurisdiction ≠ hotel physical location.
4. Preserve credible incidental finds outside the five priority markets.
5. Expand productive free/permitted source families internationally without new country-specific authorization.
6. Prioritize depth in the five markets, but allocate by verified incremental yield.
7. Maintain country-level metrics for **all** countries.
8. No country allowlists, geographic rejection gates, or country-specific opportunity ceilings.
9. Continue beyond 1,000 / 2,500 / 5,000 / 10,000 whenever evidence and authorized economics support it.
10. Verify each hotel’s **physical** country as data quality — never as eligibility restriction.

## Dual scorecard

| View | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Global Opportunity Inventory** | All countries, all qualified opportunities, unlimited expansion |
| **Priority Market Performance** | Enhanced US / MX / BR / GB / CA concentration |

## Success criterion

Build a continuously expanding worldwide hotel development intelligence engine while developing particularly strong commercial coverage in five initial markets.
