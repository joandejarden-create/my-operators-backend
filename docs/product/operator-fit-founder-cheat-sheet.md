# Operator Fit — Founder Cheat Sheet

### What Operator Fit does
Ranks hotel operators for a **specific project** using Dealality’s **verified** operator universe. It is a **project-fit** assessment — not a forecast of financial performance, fees, brand approval, or operator interest.

### Main scoring layers
1. **Operator–Project Alignment (70%)** — 8 factors (geography 22, segment 14, asset/development 20, complexity 12, brand experience 10, governance 10, regional 6, commercial differentiator 6)  
2. **Operating Structure Alignment (15%)**  
3. **Brand–Operator Compatibility (15%)** — skipped if no preferred brands  

Then subtract **Execution Risk** (max 25 points).

### Eligibility (who can appear)
Hard gates remove operators (e.g. required **Market Presence** missing).  
**Strong geo:** Current Managed Property · Current Operating Portfolio · Regional Office/Team.  
Strategic Interest / Historical / Claimed Capability **do not** establish current eligibility alone.  
Survivors may be **Eligible With Conditions** → owner label **Potential Fit — Validation Needed**.

### Missing-data rule
Unknown applicable factors contribute **0** but **stay in the denominator**. Factors are not reweighted. Sparse profiles cannot inflate scores.

### Evidence Strength
**Strong / Moderate / Limited** from evidence quality + how much applicable weight is known.  
Operator-reported alone **cannot** be Strong.

### Score ceiling (Displayed Alignment)
Limited ≤ **69** · Moderate ≤ **84** · Strong **uncapped** (0–100 clamp).

### Alignment bands (owner headline)
Strong ≥70 · Good ≥55 · Potential ≥40 · Limited &lt;40.  
Owners see the **band first** — not `/100` (Option C).

### Why results differ
Eligibility → Displayed score → Evidence Strength → Coverage → lower risk → stable ID tie-break.  
Production list keeps **Ranking Ready** only (critical fields + **≥50%** coverage).

### Validate Next
The single most important next confirmation (eligibility-first), then outreach / commercial items. Deterministic — not AI-invented.

### Owner View vs Advisor
**Owner:** band, Evidence Strength, why, concern, Validate Next, shortlist; number in expand.  
**Advisor:** adds coverage, eligibility, readiness, factor breakdown, diagnostics. Same underlying evaluation.

### What remains for outreach
Project-specific brand approval · operator interest/capacity · fees/terms · structure confirmation when unknown · regional resources when unconfirmed.
