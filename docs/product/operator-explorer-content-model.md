# Operator Explorer — Content Model

**Date:** 2026-08-09  
**Status:** Draft  
**UI today:** tabbed Operator Explorer + gold mock — evolve toward these owner-useful sections without exposing internal diagnostics.

---

## Owner-facing sections

### Overview
Who they are: identity, role (third-party / owner-operator / brand-managed), scale snapshot, HQ, parent, one honest positioning paragraph.

### Operating Footprint
Where they operate: countries/markets with **presence type** honesty (current portfolio vs office vs development vs interest).

### Portfolio Profile
What types of hotels: chain scales, service models, urban/resort mix, keys bands — prefer assignment-derived summaries.

### Relevant Experience
Development/project experience: new-build, conversion, reflag, turnaround, repositioning — evidenced, not checklist theater.

### Brand Relationships
Systems/brands they demonstrably work with — **experience labeled**; approval only if explicitly evidenced; never infer global approval.

### Selected Assignments
Actual hotel examples (from Assignments and/or Case Studies). Property name, location, brand, situation, why relevant.

### Operating Structures
How they work with owners: third-party management, franchise support, brand-managed adjacency, hybrids.

### Differentiating Capabilities
Only meaningful differentiators with evidence. Suppress generic RM/sales “we do everything” as hero content.

### Market Presence
Current operating depth (typed). Separate from strategic interest.

### Recent Momentum
Recent openings, agreements, expansion, transactions when useful and dated — operator analog to Brand Recent Momentum cards (reuse card shape, not brand writers).

### Evidence / Last Verified
Research transparency: AI-assisted/profile footnote pattern, last reviewed, source basis, region — **do not** show conflict matrices, claim IDs, or Fit scores to owners.

---

## Data ownership

### Operator Explorer owns (facts)
Identity, geography, assignments, portfolio, experience, brand relationships (non-project), structures, capabilities, evidence metadata.

### Operator Fit derives (project-specific)
Eligibility, geography score, comparable relevance, segment/development alignment, operator alignment, evidence strength for evaluation, candidate tier, Why This Operator, Validate Next.

**Never** store project-specific Fit scores on Operator Master as permanent characteristics.

---

## Internal-only (not owner sections)

- Claims conflict queues  
- Publication decision logs  
- Research Stage / Test Fixture chrome (except honest “Under Evaluation” if ever shown)  
- Advisor scorecards  
- Shortlist snapshots
