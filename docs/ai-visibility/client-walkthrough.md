# Brand AI Visibility — Client Walkthrough (15–20 min)

**Tone:** factual · observed model behavior · no preference / “AI recommends” language.

## Opening frame (say this first)

“We are looking specifically at how AI represents your brands in **hotel-owner decision scenarios** across the selected geography — not general consumer search visibility. We test the questions owners and developers actually ask when choosing a brand.”

## Presenter sequence

1. **Executive Intelligence tiles** — Up to five evidence-backed takeaways (competitive gaps, Potential AI Perception Gap, Observed AI Association, provider disagreement when material). Read headline → evidence line → recommended review / action disposition badge.
2. **Portfolio AI Presence** — “Share of monitored owner questions where at least one of your linked brands appeared.”
3. **Strongest / weakest brand** — Your Brands table; click into Autograph (Marriott) or Hotel Indigo (IHG).
4. **Owner Decision Scenario gaps** — From Executive Intelligence or Priority Review; distinguish **Action Required** vs **Review Required** vs **No Action Indicated** (expected positioning).
5. **Provider differences** — Start **OpenAI**; then switch to **All Providers** (derived cross-provider view — not a fifth model). Explain: “OpenAI is one perspective; we also monitor Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude to see whether the pattern is consistent.”
6. **Questions Missing** — Count + open watchlist; clarify absence ≠ automatic poor fit; out-of-scope scenarios are not executive problems.
7. **Peer context** — Competitive position / peer gaps (provider-specific rank).
8. **Sources** — Citation coverage; owned vs external; recurring domains (**cited / associated** — not causal).
9. **Language** — EN vs ES when comparable; Language Comparison module intentionally shows both.
10. **Discoverability** — Separate from Presence; “Last checked”; not a composite score.
11. **Detailed View** — Brand Overview → Owner Decision Scenario grouping → Competitive Gaps → Observed Associations → AI vs Dealality Context (Truth) → Watchlist.
12. **Evidence** — Open one gap, one association, and one Truth comparison; show response → prompt → supporting span → citations.

## Expectation setting (say this)

- Results are **observed model behavior** and can vary by run and provider.
- **All Providers** is a derived comparison, not a combined AI run.
- **Discoverability** is public-URL baseline — separate from AI Presence.
- **Potential AI Perception Gap** compares observed AI claims to governed Brand Basics — not “AI is wrong.”
- **Observed AI Association** is validated co-occurrence in responses — not recommendation validation.
- Monitoring is **manually governed** today (not continuous / biweekly automation).
- **Recommendation Share / Questions Won / First Recommendation / Opportunity Score** are not in V1.
- **Census-backed footprint truth** and **narrative/source causal intelligence** are deferred post-pilot.
