# GDI 3.0 — Founder Correction: Complete Intelligence in Every Full E2E Run

**Date:** 2026-11-19  
**Status:** Binding architectural clarification  
**Applies to:** Slice 2A persistence contract · upcoming Full E2E certification · all later GDI 3.0 slices  
**Does not authorize:** Paid research · production Airtable writes · customer publication · expanding PR #444 into all pillars

## Clarification

Prior “weekly” examples illustrated **intelligence accumulation**, not separate research phases.

**Every authorized GDI Full E2E execution for a hotel must attempt the complete applicable intelligence lifecycle during that same run.**

Target architecture:

> One hotel. One integrated GDI Full E2E run. Complete applicable intelligence. Durable persistence. Accurate Weekly Change. Actionable opportunity briefs.

## Development phases vs production execution

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Development phases** | Order in which we **build and certify** capabilities (Slice 1 → 2A → 2B → …). Dependencies still justify phased delivery. |
| **Production execution** | One **integrated** Full E2E process that performs **all applicable** capabilities during each authorized run. |

Do **not** build independent weekly pipelines for competitive research, contacts, or partners. Integrate into the canonical GDI research lifecycle.

## Lifecycle dimensions (required evaluation each applicable run)

1. Discovery across all 14 demand segments  
2. Lookup and reuse of existing Airtable / durable intelligence  
3. Demand evidence and commercial qualification  
4. Verified named contacts and commercial entry points  
5. Partners, planners, intermediaries (where relevant)  
6. Prior placements, recurring events, competitive intelligence (where relevant)  
7. Forward-looking demand and sourcing windows  
8. Hotel-specific fit, opportunity, pursuit recommendations  
9. Evidence-backed Commercial Intelligence Brief  
10. Durable create/update of research, org, opportunity, contact, partner intelligence  
11. Weekly Change vs prior durable state  
12. Customer publication-readiness under existing authorization gates  

Machine contract: `lib/group-demand-intelligence/workflow/gdi-full-e2e-complete-intelligence-lifecycle-contract-v1.js`

## Research depth

- Evaluate all applicable dimensions in the run.  
- Evaluating ≠ unlimited search.  
- Reuse verified evidence; target unresolved commercial questions; respect authorized budgets.  
- Record explicit outcomes: `VERIFIED` · `REUSED` · `NOT_FOUND` · `NOT_APPLICABLE` · `RESEARCH_EXHAUSTED` · `BLOCKED_BY_BUDGET` (+ `PERSISTENCE_FAILED` / `NOT_YET_WIRED` during build-out).  
- Distinguish **attempted** from **successfully verified**.

## Persistence and reuse

- Before research: check durable intelligence and stable identities.  
- After research: persist complete permissible intelligence; record failures.  
- Do not defer available contacts / partners / competitor evidence to a future run.  
- Do **not** report durable completion when required persistence failed.

## Weekly Change

- Computed as part of **every applicable** Full E2E run.  
- First run establishes baseline; later runs compare to that baseline.  
- Evidence-based and stable across reruns.  
- Repeated opportunity without material change must **not** be `NEW` or `UPDATED`.

2. **Do not** expand the current PR into all GDI 3.0 pillars.  
3. Document capabilities that cannot yet complete in one run → prioritized backlog.  
4. No paid research / production writes / publication without separate founder authorization.
