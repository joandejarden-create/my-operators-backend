## Default source hierarchy

1. Official operator property and portfolio pages  
2. Official operator corporate website  
3. Official hotel website  
4. Official owner or developer website  
5. Official brand hotel page  
6. Official brand development information  
7. Official operator press release  
8. Official brand press release  
9. Owner, developer, lender, investor, or government announcement  
10. Reliable hospitality trade publication  
11. Reliable local business or development publication  
12. Professional profiles for team and organizational facts  
13. Other sources only as supporting evidence

## Standing rules

- Search-result snippets are never evidence.  
- AI summaries are never evidence.  
- Automatically generated directories are not primary evidence.  
- An undated source cannot confirm time-sensitive current status without corroboration.  
- One property does not prove portfolio-wide capability.  
- One announced project does not prove operating presence.  
- One historical relationship does not prove current approval.  
- Marketing language remains **Operator Reported** unless independently supported.

## Market Presence Type (controlled)

Use exactly:

- Current Managed Property  
- Current Operating Portfolio  
- Regional Office or Team  
- Active Development  
- Historical Presence  
- Strategic Interest  
- Claimed Capability  
- Unknown  

Do not treat all presence types as equivalent for eligibility.
