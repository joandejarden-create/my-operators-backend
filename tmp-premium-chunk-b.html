Frequently asked questions

Find clear, straightforward answers about how Dealality supports hotel owners evaluating brands, operators, conversion, and strategic partners.

1. Who is Dealality for?

Hotel owners evaluating brand, operator, conversion, or strategic-partner decisions—and the brands, operators, and advisors who participate in those processes.

2. Do I need an account to start?

No. You can begin with a confidential opportunity review without creating an account.

3. How is information shared?

Sensitive information is organized and shared according to the needs of the process. The owner remains in control of which parties advance.

4. Is Dealality a marketplace that auto-matches deals?

No. Dealality structures research, engagement, and comparison. Hospitality judgment and owner decisions remain central.

5. What markets are in focus?

Dealality is rolling out with focus across Caribbean and Latin America, Canada, Europe, and North America.
