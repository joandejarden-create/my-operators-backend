# Website Restructure Brief (PREPARE ONLY — no build)

**Authority:** JD-6B-002 · Class B from Phase 6B  
**Source:** dealality.com audit 2026-09-07

## Current hierarchy problem
Homepage sells confidential selection/path-compare as the whole company. ADP (major commercial product) is effectively absent. Dual-track GTM is not visible in 5 seconds.

## Proposed homepage hierarchy
1. Owner stakes (CLEAR → STAKES)
2. What Dealality helps with today (hotel dealmaking in plain language)
3. Two entry paths: **Live decision** | **Operating hotel / ADP**
4. Proof / honesty about pilot stage
5. CTA architecture
6. Brand/operator paths (secondary, maturity-gated)

## Owner story
Dealality helps hotel owners make and manage better hotel deals — evaluate, structure, compare, manage decisions and counterparties confidentially (D1).

## Stakes
Missed alternatives · weak leverage · fragmented process · AI answers omitting/misrepresenting the hotel (ADP).

## ADP placement
Equal first-class entry for operating/opening hotels — not buried. Pilot CTA with MARKET-TEST label when commercial.

## Dealmaking placement
Trigger-based path: Explorers/compare/process as workflow, not every module equal.

## Brand/operator paths
Keep role cards; subscription/enterprise claims maturity-gated; ADP demo without invented prices.

## Proof architecture
Honest pilot stage; no fake cases; founder credibility; verify testimonial provenance.

## CTA architecture
SEE HOW IT WORKS · REQUEST A DEMO · DISCUSS A PILOT. Retire Beta Access.

## Keep / Change / Create / Remove
- **Keep:** confidential owner-control language; FAQ quality; role structure
- **Change:** hero hierarchy; CTAs; pricing section honesty vs D5 taxonomy
- **Create:** ADP entry module; dual-path chooser
- **Remove/deprioritize:** feature equality; beta CTA; overbroad ecosystem claims

## Claims that must change
- Any implication Dealality = selection-only
- Any beta-primary CTA
- Any ROI/booking guarantee
- Marketplace-as-identity
- Unverified testimonials until proven
