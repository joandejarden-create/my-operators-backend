## JW Marriott (`jw-marriott`)

- **Core identity:** Marriott luxury hard brand — polished luxury full service
- **Owner proposition:** Luxury Bonvoy brand between Marriott Hotels and ultra-ritual Ritz-Carlton/St. Regis
- **Typical asset fit:** Luxury urban hotels and resorts with substantial public space
- **Service model:** Luxury full service
- **Experience differentiator:** Polished Marriott luxury — not Ritz-Carlton legendary service ritual or EDITION lifestyle
- **Development differentiator:** Luxury capital and operating intensity above Marriott Hotels
- **Conversion / new-build:** Both — high capital either path
- **Contamination siblings:** Marriott Hotels, The Ritz-Carlton, St. Regis, EDITION
- **Must NOT migrate language from:** Marriott Hotels upper-upscale flagship; Ritz-Carlton ladies-and-gentlemen ritual; St. Regis butler ritual; EDITION lifestyle luxury
