Where wellness is built in

With thoughtful options for rest, nutrition, exercise and productivity, EVEN Hotels appeals to wellness-minded business travellers who want to be at their best while on the road. Defying the traditional expectation that travel disrupts your wellness and fitness regimes, the brand is reaching new destinations, including recent key openings in Greater China, and is uniquely positioned to capitalise on the opportunities in a burgeoning wellness market.

All about our guests

Rather than letting their wellness and fitness routines lapse while on the road, guests of EVEN Hotels want to incorporate wellness into their trips, so they can continue to keep active, rest easy, eat well and accomplish more every day. Frequent travellers, they appreciate the brand’s thoughtfully designed rooms, which have soothing colour palettes, fresh scents and natural bedding to promote stress-free living, along with ergonomic workstations to boost productivity. With a balanced approach to healthy living, they exercise three to four times a week and value being empowered to make healthy choices through the brand’s fitness classes, well-equipped gyms, wellness-savvy staff and array of healthy eating options.

A unique offer for owners

At the heart of the upscale brand’s success is a unique customer-centric offer that recognises that wellness is now a trend for millions of consumers. An efficient design prototype incorporates the brand’s signature flexible and invigorating spaces, which balance energy and calm, while its efficient service model carefully controls operating costs. As well as being part of a growing market segment, owners benefit from IHG’s powerful systems, global scale and leading IHG One Rewards loyalty programme.
