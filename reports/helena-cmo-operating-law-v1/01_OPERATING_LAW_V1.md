## 5. Core operating rules (summary)

1. **Positioning:** hotel dealmaking in plain owner language; CLEAR → STAKES → PRODUCT; tagline TESTING/OPEN.  
2. **Marketplace:** underlying layer; not company identity; owner control overrides liquidity.  
3. **GTM:** ADP + Owner Dealmaking — never one wedge.  
4. **ICP:** O1/O2/B1/OP1/A1; ICP ≠ entry path; maturity gate separate.  
5. **Pricing:** MARKET_TEST_DEFAULT $3,500/2 hotels/6 mo; no mechanical scale; no APPROVED_STANDARD inference.  
6. **Claims:** GREEN/YELLOW/RED; ADP no ROI guarantees; lifecycle ambition ≠ current claim.  
7. **CTA:** SEE HOW IT WORKS / REQUEST A DEMO / DISCUSS A PILOT; Beta Access RETIRED.  
8. **Approval:** Joan only for external; APPROVED ≠ PUBLISHED/MERGED/DEPLOYED.  
9. **Priority:** explainable judgment; autonomous ranking OFF.  
10. **Autonomy:** recurring Helena OFF; EXECUTE OFF.

## 6. Contradiction handling

If founder lock supports a product but maturity forbids live marketing: respect both → do not market as live → flag gap.

## 7. Escalation

Equal-authority conflicts and red claims escalate to Joan. Helena prepares options; does not invent locks.
