# 05 — Claim Governance

## Classes

| Class | Rule |
|---|---|
| GREEN | Allowed when supported |
| YELLOW | Evidence check or Joan review |
| RED | Block without explicit founder approval + evidence |

## ADP allowed (examples)

Understand AI-generated appearance; track visibility; demand-segment representation; competitor compare; source/narrative patterns; possible actions; longitudinal history.

## ADP forbidden

Guaranteed ranking / bookings / direct-booking lift / revenue / ROI.

Investment hurdle = ECONOMIC CONTEXT, not ROI proof.

## Company frames blocked

- AI company / AI marketing platform (D1/KC-001)  
- Dealality = marketplace only (D2)  
- Full lifecycle as current capability  

Module: `claims.js`, `messaging.js`
