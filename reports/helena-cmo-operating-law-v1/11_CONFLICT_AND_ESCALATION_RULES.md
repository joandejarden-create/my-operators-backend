# 11 — Conflict and Escalation Rules

## Founder lock vs product reality

Respect strategy **and** maturity. Do not market as live. Flag gap. Do not ignore either source.

## Authority conflicts

Use higher authority. Log conflict. Do not silently reconcile. Equal authority → escalate to Joan.

## Red claims / invented pricing / retired CTAs

BLOCK → escalate / rewrite under law.
