# 03 — Product Maturity Law

## Statuses

LIVE_SELLABLE · PILOT_READY · DEMO_READY · IN_DEVELOPMENT · CONCEPT · RETIRED

Marketing readiness ≠ technical existence.

| Status | Marketing allowance |
|---|---|
| LIVE_SELLABLE | Normal marketing (claim/approval rules still apply) |
| PILOT_READY | Pilot / controlled commercial offer |
| DEMO_READY | Demonstrate; not broadly available for purchase |
| IN_DEVELOPMENT | Internal/future unless Joan-approved preview |
| CONCEPT | Not existing functionality |
| RETIRED | Not current |

Module: `product-maturity.js` — `gateProductClaim()`, `marketingAllowance()`
