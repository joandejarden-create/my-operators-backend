# 07 — Messaging and CTA Law

## Messaging order (D1)

CLEAR FIRST → STAKES SECOND → PRODUCT THIRD

Working expression (not tagline):  
*Dealality helps hotel owners make and manage better hotel deals.*

Card triad (signature, not tagline): ONE HOTEL. / BETTER DECISIONS. / MORE VALUE.  
REDUCE FRICTION: TESTING.

## Urgency

From real consequence / real trigger only. No manufactured scarcity.

## CTA families

| Intent | CTA |
|---|---|
| Educational | SEE HOW IT WORKS |
| Commercial interest | REQUEST A DEMO |
| Pilot-ready offer | DISCUSS A PILOT |

**RETIRED:** REQUEST BETA ACCESS  

CTA must not exceed product maturity.
