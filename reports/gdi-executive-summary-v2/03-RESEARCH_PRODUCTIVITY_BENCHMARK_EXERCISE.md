# Repeatable Manual Benchmark Exercise — GDI Research Productivity

**Purpose:** Validate or revise provisional activity benchmarks before presenting them as validated-conservative customer facts.  
**Status:** Exercise prepared — not yet executed as a timed founder study in this cycle.

## Protocol

1. Select representative opportunities across:
   - Corporate / scaling accounts
   - Associations / conferences
   - Competitive demand
   - Construction / projects
   - Leisure / sports (when present)
2. For each opportunity, a trained hotel-sales researcher (not using Dealality outputs) performs the work needed to reach **equivalent** commercial intelligence:
   - Organization / event understanding
   - Hotel-fit judgment
   - Decision-maker identification
   - Professional contact channel
   - Recommended next sales action
3. Time **active research only** (exclude meetings, CRM logging, and pursuit outreach).
4. Record completeness vs canonical GDI fields and quality notes.
5. Compare measured minutes to provisional low / base / high assumptions.
6. Promote customer-facing reporting to `VALIDATED_CONSERVATIVE` only after founder acceptance of revised benchmarks.

## Guardrails
