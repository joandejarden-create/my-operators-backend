# Hotel Lease Intelligence — Deferred Capability (V5)

## Founder direction (binding for current HIF V5)

**Prioritize Franchise Agreement Intelligence and Management Contract Intelligence.**

Lease Intelligence is a future enhancement and must not receive dedicated research expenditure or development priority at this stage.

| Rule | Status |

| Delay franchise / management discovery for lease work | **No** |

## Reassessment

Reassess Lease Intelligence only after commercial value of Brand Conversion and Management Contract services is established.

## Immediate objective (unchanged)

Maximize verified global **Brand Conversion Targets** and **Management Contract Targets**, including historical and future expirations, notice periods, ownership intelligence, and relevant decision-makers.
