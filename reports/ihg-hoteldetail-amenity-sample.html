Santo Domingo’s Business District Hotel

Located in the heart of Santo Domingo’s Business District, the renovated Holiday Inn® Santo Domingo is ideal for business travelers. Top companies like Coca-Cola, Microsoft, and Unilever are minutes away offering convenient access for meetings and corporate events. Get free Wi-Fi, parking, a 24/7 Business Centre, and seven versatile meeting spaces.

Explore popular nearby attractions, including Ágora Mall, Blue Mall, Quisqueya Stadium, and the National Botanical Garden. Our on-site restaurants offer delicious dining, along with cocktails during Happy Hour.

Relax with amenities like a rooftop pool, Fitness Centre, and sauna. End your day in our spacious, pet-friendly suites with plush bedding and flat-screen TVs.
