## Differentiation vs conventional tools

**Working differentiation (CONFIRMED capability):** hotel-specific packages, shared ten-section drawers, CMI vs Hotel Market split, publication fingerprints, founder approval gates, contact-bearing cards.

**Not yet proven as durable moat:** systematically earlier discovery than event databases; multi-provider complementarity; weekly material-change history.

## Discovery measurement (recommended methodology)

Use existing verified packages as fixtures:

1. Known-opportunity recall against frozen CURRENT IDs.  
2. New unique qualified count per authorized run (ledger).  
3. Forward-looking lead time (eventStartDate − discovery date).  
4. Evidence reliability + contact role class.  
5. Dedup collision rate.  
6. Cost per new customer-visible opp (Exa/Apify ledgers).  
7. Hotel acceptance / feedback when granted.

Do **not** equate more paid spend with better discovery without these denominators.
