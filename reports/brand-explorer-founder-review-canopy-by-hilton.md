## Brand positioning

Canopy by Hilton is a Hilton lifestyle / neighborhood-oriented boutique brand within Hilton. For owners, the affiliation case is lifestyle positioning, local experience cues, upper-upscale fit, and Hilton Honors platform participation. It should be evaluated on its own guest promise and operating implications, not as a generic parent-brand substitute or an interchangeable peer among Curio Collection, Tapestry Collection by Hilton, Tempo by Hilton, Motto by Hilton.
