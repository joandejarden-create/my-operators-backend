## 1. Where Dealality stands (company context)

Dealality is building a confidential hotel owner decision platform: the place an owner goes when brand, operator, demand, or deal structure questions are real and expensive. The primary buyer is the hotel owner / owner-side advisor — not the brand sales team and not the general travel consumer.

What is commercially usable today is uneven:

- **Owner dealmaking identity** is the public spine (Insights on soft brands, key money, HMA vs franchise, brand development). That identity matches the founder’s expertise and the ICP’s language.
- **Product surfaces** exist and are partially public: AI Demand Positioning (ADP) has a live public page; Brand Explorer, Brand AI Visibility, dashboards, and login paths appear in GA4. Operator Explorer and census-scale systems are stronger as product/data assets than as self-serve GTM engines.
- **Near-term business need** is not “more awareness.” It is a **closed commercial learning loop**: named warm accounts → clear paid-pilot offer → measurable form/demo/pilot events → paid continuation. Without that loop, every channel debate is theatrical.

Marketing’s job right now is not to make Dealality famous. It is to make the next five to fifteen serious owner conversations inevitable, measurable, and economically intelligible.

Historically, Helena/AO/Cursor built a Marketing OS, SEO cadence, LinkedIn publishing, Insights inventory, and strategy drafts while measurement of live channels was incomplete. That era is over for channel truth: **GA4 and GSC are live via Enrich**. What remains incomplete is commercial outcome measurement.

## 2. What the live numbers actually say

### 2.1 Traffic contracted while search “visibility” exploded

| Window | GA4 sessions |
|--------|-------------:|
| Last 7 days | **25** |
| Last 30 days | **97** |
| Previous 30 days | **241** |
| Last 90 days | **518** |

**What happened:** Latest-30d sessions are down **−144 (−60%)** vs prior 30d.

**Why it may be happening:** The prior 30d likely included denser Organic Social / founder-network / referral spikes (90d Organic Social alone is 116 sessions). The latest 30d looks like a quieter relationship month plus a still-thin organic search contribution. Direct remains the majority in both windows — this is not “SEO died”; it is “non-search volume cooled while search never carried the system.”

**What it proves:** Dealality’s website demand is still small and volatile. Month-to-month swings of this size on a ~100–250 session base are expected when traffic is relationship-driven.

**What it does not prove:** It does not prove the product is unwanted, nor that LinkedIn “failed,” nor that SEO “succeeded.” Absolute levels are too low for those conclusions.

**Why Joan should care:** If you manage to “content volume” or “posting cadence” without watching this volatility, you will celebrate activity during quiet commercial months and panic during normal relationship lulls.

**Strategy meaning:** Do not build a GTM plan that requires 1,000+ monthly sessions to work. Build a plan that works at **relationship scale** and treats site traffic as a secondary signal.

**What happened:** The site is primarily reached by people who already know the URL or arrive via dark/direct paths. Search is not the engine. Social and ChatGPT-class assistants are detectable.

**What it does not prove:** It does not prove LinkedIn ROI, assistant ROI, or that Direct is “brand strength” rather than “internal + repeat + dark social.” Returning users are only **6 active users** driving 29 returning sessions — a tiny loyal core, not a broad brand.

**Strategy meaning:** Double down on **named-account activation** and **offer clarity**. Treat SEO as a hygiene + selective intent play, not the growth thesis.

### 2.4 Conversion events stop at form_start

Events last 30d include `form_start = 8`. Named demo/pilot events are **NOT_FOUND** in the stream. `click = 2` is negligible.

**What happened:** We can see people begin forms. We cannot see completions, demo requests, pilot requests, or revenue events in GA4.

**What it proves:** Some conversion UI is being touched (~8% of sessions if naively ratioed — treat carefully on small n).

**What it does not prove:** Pipeline creation from web. Commercial funnel health.

**Strategy meaning:** Until form_complete → conversation → demo → pilot are events (and preferably joined to GTM identities), Helena cannot honestly score “digital GTM effectiveness.” This is a **NOW** priority, not a nice-to-have analytics chore.

### 2.5 Search: impressions surged; clicks barely moved; mix is commercially polluted

| Window | Clicks | Impressions | CTR |
|--------|-------:|------------:|----:|
| Last 7d | 3 | 497 | 0.60% |
| Last 28d | 6 | 1,376 | 0.44% |
| Prior 28d | 2 | 114 | 1.75% |
| Last 90d | 12 | 1,551 | 0.77% |

Query-class sample (28d): **LOW VALUE ~264 impressions / 0 clicks** (branded-residence cluster) dwarfs **HOTEL DEALMAKING 13i**, **BRAND SELECTION 35i**, **ADP-AI 55i**, **OPERATOR SELECTION 3i**. Branded Dealality search is almost nonexistent (2 impressions, 1 click in sample).

**Why impressions rose while clicks stayed tiny — Helena’s call: combination B + C + D + E**

- **B Wrong queries:** Branded-residence / suite-ownership adjacent queries are not Dealality’s ICP buying moment.
- **C Poor rankings:** Many commercial queries sit deep (positions 30–80+).
- **D Weak SERP proposition:** Even when shown, title/meta/intent match likely fails to earn the click against incumbents.
- **E Irrelevant content exposure:** Residences content is manufacturing visibility without commercial click intent.

This is **not** primarily “A useful early search visibility” in the sense founders hope for. Some early useful visibility exists (soft brands, key money pages get a few clicks; CALA brand-selection-ish queries appear at better positions with tiny volume). The **bulk of the impression spike is not a win**.

**Strategy meaning:** SEO posture = **REFESH / REFOCUS**, not “invest harder in the same content machine.”

## 17. Conversion architecture — what happens after interest

Map the intended commercial path against live evidence:

**Content / outreach → website.** LinkedIn contributes a measurable but small ATTRIBUTED path (8/30d). Insights pages contribute mid-single-digit sessions. Enrich/agent and Clarity referrals appear in acquisition grain. This stage is partially measurable.

**Website → product interest.** ADP and Brand Explorer/Brand AI paths appear. ADP’s low bounce and high pages/session among two active users is the strongest product-interest micro-signal on the site. This stage is partially measurable via page paths, not via explicit “product_interest” events.

**Product interest → form.** `form_start = 8` exists. That is real. It is also where analytics largely stop.

**Form → conversation → demo → pilot → paid → expansion.** In GA4, demo/pilot named events are NOT_FOUND. In GTM, Call Completed = 5 and Replied = 1 against Connected = 58. Paid and expansion are not visible in the live marketing metrics pack.

**So what:** Dealality does not currently have a digital conversion architecture; it has a **website plus a CRM-ish list** with a dark middle. The middle is where money is decided. Until that middle is instrumented and operated, channel debates are mostly aesthetic.
