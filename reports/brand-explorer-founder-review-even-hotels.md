## Brand positioning

EVEN Hotels is a wellness-led upscale select / lifestyle hotel brand within IHG Hotels & Resorts. For owners, the affiliation case is wellness positioning, conversion or new-build prototype fit, IHG distribution and loyalty, operating model implications, and market demand for wellness-minded travelers. It should be evaluated on its own guest promise and operating implications, not as a generic parent-brand substitute or an interchangeable peer among Holiday Inn Express, avid hotels, voco, Hotel Indigo.
