Fact Sheet

Location

Set between the Lalinde and Provenza neighborhoods, the hotel offers a wide range of nearby options for shopping and dining. The Provenza area of El Poblado is one of the city’s most vibrant social hubs, known for its boutiques, diverse culinary and nightlife scene, art spaces, and local energy. A public park beside the hotel creates an open and lively environment.

Design & Culture

Architecture Architecture Harmony and innovation

Architecture Architecture Harmony and innovation

Defined by character, permanence, and silence, the hotel offers 360° views of the mountains and the city.

Wake Medellin is built on a platform that complements the existing commercial area, maintaining harmony with its surroundings through angular geometry and floating slabs. Its levels replicate a typical orthogonal floor plan that shifts in position as it rises, giving the appearance of an undulating structure. A linear park created on the second floor establishes a connection between the Provenza neighborhood and the hotel.

Interior design Design Cinco Solidos

The hotel’s serene, minimalist design was inspired by Japanese and Nordic aesthetics, with cement, wood, and metal serving as its defining elements.

The honest, essential material palette features exposed concrete, natural wood, and stone, with calm neutral tones enhanced by deep burgundy accents that add emotional and sophisticated contrasts. Within this simplicity, touches of color and form emerge to elevate the experience through unique design pieces, unexpected lines, and subtle textures that enrich the narrative without breaking the coherence of the whole.

Food & Drink

Contemporary bistro Boro

Enjoy fine dining created by one of the world’s Top 50 chefs.

Boro offers a bold, contemporary take on Colombian ingredients, with a seasonal menu designed by top chef Jaime Rodríguez that changes based on local harvests. The hotel also presents a private Omakase—Japanese-style dining where the chef prepares a multicourse meal for up to eight guests—as well as a curated wine cellar.

Wellness

Social wellness Where connections flourish

Discover a wide range of wellness-focused facilities.

Wake stands out for its social wellness concept. Guests can enjoy three pools at different temperatures, an infrared sauna, a flotation therapy experience, a hammam that’s perfect for massages, a yoga and Pilates studio, and a silence and meditation spot. There is also a fully equipped 24-hour gym.
