## Exact decision text recorded

D4 ICP V1 — FOUNDER_LOCKED. This is the CURRENT OPERATING ICP MODEL for Helena. It is not a permanent statement that these are the only clients Dealality may ever serve. Dealality historically identified a broader stakeholder ecosystem. For the current stage, Helena must focus normal GTM attention on the client groups below. 1. STRATEGIC PRIMARY CLIENT — HOTEL OWNERS / OWNERSHIP GROUPS: governing ICP principle. Why: owners control or heavily influence major hotel decisions; owners bear economic consequences; Dealality is designed around owner control and confidentiality; owner adoption can pull other stakeholders into the platform; long-term ambition centers on supporting the owner across more of the hotel lifecycle. This does NOT mean every Dealality product must only be sold to owners. 2. DEVELOPERS: included alongside owners when acting in an owner/developer decision-making capacity (new development; brand selection; operator selection; positioning; pre-opening; future ownership/economic interest). Do not create artificial separation between owner and developer when the same buying/problem logic applies. 3. CURRENT OWNER OPERATING SEGMENTS: OWNER SEGMENT A — LIVE HOTEL DEALMAKING DECISION: real decision such as brand/operator selection, reflag, HMA/operator review, repositioning, new-build brand/operator decision, or comparable owner-controlled hotel dealmaking supported by current product maturity. Primary entry: OWNER DECISION WORKFLOW. Possible capabilities (maturity-gated): Brand Explorer; Operator Explorer; Brand Alignment Snapshot; Operator Alignment Snapshot; Deal Readiness; comparison / decision support; owner-controlled outreach / next steps. Priority: HIGH WHEN TRIGGER EXISTS. Do not manufacture urgency if there is no real decision. OWNER SEGMENT B — OPERATING / OPENING HOTEL WITH ADP RELEVANCE: owner/ownership group with operating hotels, opening hotels, or portfolio assets where AI-generated hotel discovery / recommendation / positioning is commercially relevant. Primary entry: AI DEMAND POSITIONING. Does NOT require a current brand/operator decision. Engage reasons may include: how the hotel appears in AI-generated answers; competitive visibility; demand-segment representation; owner reporting; source / narrative gaps; ongoing monitoring. Priority: ACTIVE COMMERCIAL PRIORITY. Do not require proof of a pre-existing “AI visibility gap” before the account can qualify — the diagnostic itself may establish the gap. 4. BRANDS — ACTIVE SECONDARY COMMERCIAL CLIENT now (not merely future). Current possible commercial entry: ADP; Brand AI Visibility; hotel / portfolio AI representation intelligence. Ecosystem role may include: participation in owner brand-selection processes; qualified opportunity participation; matching / fit; future marketplace / ecosystem workflows. Distinguish BRAND AS CUSTOMER vs BRAND AS PARTICIPANT IN AN OWNER WORKFLOW. Priority: ACTIVE SECONDARY COMMERCIAL. Do not promote immature enterprise SKUs as ready. 5. OPERATORS — ACTIVE SECONDARY COMMERCIAL CLIENT now. Current possible commercial entry: ADP at property level; ADP at portfolio level; future operator intelligence / visibility products where mature. Ecosystem role may include: participation in owner operator-selection processes; operator comparison; alignment; qualified opportunity participation; future marketplace / ecosystem workflows. Distinguish OPERATOR AS CUSTOMER vs OPERATOR AS PARTICIPANT IN AN OWNER WORKFLOW. Priority: ACTIVE SECONDARY COMMERCIAL. Do not promote immature enterprise SKUs as ready. 6. ADVISORS / LAWYERS / CONSULTANTS — P1 CHANNEL / AMPLIFIER. May introduce owners; create owner opt-in; participate in owner workflows; support adoption; strengthen credibility; help identify qualified opportunities. NOT automatically the primary economic buyer, central Dealality ICP, or substitute for owner acquisition. Support their role; do not position Dealality as replacing them. 7. OTHER STAKEHOLDER TYPES: preserve in strategic stakeholder universe. For normal near-term Helena GTM: P2 / OPPORTUNITY-LED / LATER unless a current mature product applies, a real commercial opportunity exists, Joan explicitly prioritizes, or evidence justifies moving forward. Do not delete or retire those groups. 8. ICP ≠ PRODUCT ENTRY: ICP answers WHO matters; ENTRY PATH answers WHAT current problem/product gets them into Dealality. Examples: OWNER + LIVE SELECTION TRIGGER → owner decision workflow; OWNER + ADP RELEVANCE → ADP; BRAND + ADP / AI VISIBILITY NEED → ADP / Brand AI path; OPERATOR + PROPERTY/PORTFOLIO ADP NEED → ADP; ADVISOR + QUALIFIED OWNER RELATIONSHIP → amplifier / owner opt-in path. A client may have MULTIPLE valid entry paths over time. Do not permanently assign one product to one ICP. 9. OPERATING PRIORITY: STRATEGIC P1 = Owners / ownership groups + Developers in owner-like capacity; ACTIVE SECONDARY COMMERCIAL = Brands + Operators; P1 CHANNEL / AMPLIFIER = Advisors / Lawyers / Consultants / trusted industry relationships; P2 / LATER / OPPORTUNITY-LED = other historical ecosystem stakeholder groups. 10. PRIORITIZATION SIGNALS (not separate ICPs): real decision trigger / urgency; relationship warmth; ability / willingness to pay; product maturity fit; ADP relevance; selection urgency; portfolio / expansion potential; commercial value; learning value; proof creation potential; likelihood of real adoption. 11. GEOGRAPHY: CALA preferential where founder network / market knowledge / access create advantage; NOT exclusive; no geographic hard stop. 12. HOTEL COUNT: Do NOT make 2 hotels a qualification requirement for ADP. Two hotels is the current market-test quoted configuration. Allow single hotel, two-hotel pilot, portfolio, and enterprise to be evaluated based on opportunity and pricing authority. 13. PRODUCT MATURITY GATE: strategic ICP validity ≠ product readiness. Before marketing a product to any segment verify: product maturity; marketing readiness; evidence / proof; available CTA; commercial model; delivery capacity. If insufficient: do not market broadly — classify RESEARCH / VALIDATION / DEMO / FUTURE / HOLD as appropriate. 14. WORKING ICP LABELS (INTERNAL ONLY — not public personas/website terminology): ICP-O1 Owner/Developer — Live Dealmaking Decision; ICP-O2 Owner/Ownership Group — ADP / Operating Hotel Opportunity; ICP-B1 Brand — Active Commercial / ADP Opportunity; ICP-OP1 Operator — Active Commercial / ADP Opportunity; ICP-A1 Advisor/Lawyer/Consultant — Owner Amplifier. 15. THIS LOCK DOES NOT DECIDE: permanent TAM segmentation; final enterprise packaging; enterprise brand pricing; enterprise operator pricing; final success-fee model; permanent geography focus; public website persona labels; final campaign calendar; autonomous Helena targeting; EXECUTE permissions.
