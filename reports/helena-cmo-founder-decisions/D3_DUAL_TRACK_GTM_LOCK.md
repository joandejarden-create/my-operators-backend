## Exact decision text recorded

D3 Dual-Track GTM + Ownership — FOUNDER_LOCKED. CORE: For the next 90 days, Dealality will operate TWO active GTM tracks that serve different customer situations and must not be collapsed into one wedge. TRACK 1 — AI DEMAND POSITIONING: ADP is an ACTIVE COMMERCIAL GTM PRIORITY now and a major Dealality product. ADP may generate meaningful standalone revenue, become recurring, serve as an entry point into Dealality, and create broader Dealality adoption where relevant. ADP should NOT be judged solely on whether a customer later uses another Dealality product. PRIMARY CURRENT ADP AUDIENCES: hotel owners / individual hotels; hotel ownership groups / portfolios; operators; brands. Owners remain Dealality’s strategic primary client. Brands and operators are also legitimate active commercial ADP targets — not merely future audiences. ADP NEAR-TERM OBJECTIVES (90 days): (1) real paid users (2) owner participation (3) product validation (4) recurring-use validation (5) proof creation (6) hotel coverage (7) learning what users act on (8) revenue (9) pilot-to-recurring conversion (10) broader Dealality adoption where it naturally occurs. Near-term strategy favors ADOPTION + VALIDATION + PROOF + REAL USAGE over MAXIMIZING SHORT-TERM ARPU. This does NOT mean free by default. ADP has real delivery cost and real standalone commercial value. TRACK 2 — OWNER DEALMAKING / DECISION WORKFLOW: actively support owners/developers with a LIVE hotel decision such as brand selection, operator selection, reflag, HMA / operator review, new-build brand/operator decision, repositioning-related brand/operator decision, or other current hotel-dealmaking situations supported by present product maturity. This track is TRIGGER-BASED. Do not manufacture artificial urgency where the owner does not have a real decision. When the trigger exists, this is a HIGH-PRIORITY Dealality opportunity. CURRENT PRODUCTS / CAPABILITIES MAY INCLUDE (subject to product maturity and claim governance): Brand Explorer; Operator Explorer; Brand Alignment Snapshot; Operator Alignment Snapshot; Deal Readiness / opportunity preparation; brand/operator comparison; owner-controlled outreach / next-step workflow; other current sellable or pilot-ready decision capabilities. Do not market technical existence as commercial readiness. Product maturity governs claims. ADVISORS / LAWYERS / CONSULTANTS: important as referral channels, amplifiers, introducers, and trusted participants in owner processes; should help create OWNER OPT-IN where appropriate; not automatically the primary economic buyer; Dealality supports trusted advisors rather than replacing them. GTM RELATIONSHIP: Do NOT encode ADP → mandatory selection conversion, or selection workflow → mandatory ADP conversion. Each product/workflow must be commercially valuable on its own. Helena should identify legitimate NEED-DRIVEN cross-sell/expansion where the client’s actual needs support them (e.g. ADP client develops brand/operator decision → selection may become relevant; selection client has AI visibility issue → ADP may become relevant). Cross-sell must be need-driven, not forced. COMMERCIAL OWNERSHIP — JOAN owns: founder-led selling; strategic relationships; commercial conversations; final commercial negotiation; pricing exceptions; proposals requiring founder judgment; closing; final public approval; partnership approval. No separate sales leader is required to activate this 90-day GTM model. HELENA owns CMO responsibilities including: market observation; ICP prioritization; account research; buyer/problem analysis; opportunity identification; GTM recommendations; messaging; positioning application; campaign planning; content preparation; account-specific preparation; founder sales-support materials; follow-up recommendations; proof planning; performance measurement; learning loops. Helena may PREPARE commercial material. Helena may NOT: independently negotiate; change price; promise product functionality; approve proposals; send externally without approval; close business; publish; launch campaigns. CURSOR owns technical implementation after approved handoff (website implementation; product marketing page implementation; analytics/tracking; technical fixes; approved CMO Technical Work Items). Cursor does NOT decide GTM strategy and does NOT independently modify production. WEEKLY PRIORITIZATION: Once Operating Law v1 is active, Helena should evaluate opportunities across BOTH GTM tracks. Do not automatically prioritize ADP because it is newer, or selection because it is historically core. Priority should consider: client strategic importance; real trigger / urgency; product maturity; ability to deliver; relationship warmth; willingness / ability to pay; commercial potential; learning value; proof creation; likelihood of real adoption. Founder explicit priority overrides normal ranking. GEOGRAPHY: CALA may receive preferential attention because of founder network, market knowledge, access, and historical GTM concentration — but CALA is NOT an exclusive GTM boundary. Strong qualified owner, brand, or operator opportunities elsewhere remain eligible. THIS LOCK DOES NOT DECIDE: permanent campaign calendar; final ADP standard price card; enterprise ADP pricing; final selection success-fee economics; permanent sales organization; public website copy; final tagline; autonomous Helena cadence; EXECUTE permissions — those remain governed separately.
