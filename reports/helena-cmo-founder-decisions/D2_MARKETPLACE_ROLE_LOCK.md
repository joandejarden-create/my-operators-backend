## Exact decision text recorded

D2 Marketplace Role — FOUNDER_LOCKED. CORE: Marketplace functionality remains an important part of the Dealality platform vision. However: the marketplace is NOT the public category or entire identity of Dealality. Dealality should not currently be positioned publicly as simply a “hotel marketplace.” Marketplace / matching / participation functionality should be understood as an UNDERLYING PLATFORM LAYER that supports the broader owner-driven hotel dealmaking process. WHAT MARKETPLACE MEANS INSIDE DEALALITY: owners identifying and considering relevant brands/operators/partners; brands/operators participating in qualified hotel opportunities; controlled introductions; matching / fit intelligence; structured opportunity participation; confidential information exchange; comparison of alternatives; owner-controlled outreach and engagement; future ecosystem participation across additional stakeholder groups. The marketplace may become increasingly global and broad as Dealality develops. WHAT IT DOES NOT MEAN: a public hotel-for-sale listing site; an OTA; a public deal board; unrestricted access to owner opportunities; an open database of confidential hotel deals; brokerage; transaction intermediation; a requirement that every opportunity be publicly exposed. OWNER CONTROL REMAINS GOVERNING LAW: Marketplace participation remains owner-controlled. The owner determines whether an opportunity is exposed, what information is shared, when it is shared, with whom it is shared, and whether an introduction proceeds. Confidentiality and owner control override marketplace liquidity. Dealality should not optimize marketplace activity at the expense of owner trust or confidentiality. PLATFORM ROLE: Marketplace is one layer of the broader Dealality platform. It may support Brand & Operator decision workflows, owner/developer opportunities, stakeholder participation, introductions, matching, and future lifecycle use cases. It should not restrict Dealality from developing other major product areas such as AI Demand Positioning, Owner / Hotel Intelligence, lifecycle intelligence, or other future owner-facing products. PUBLIC MESSAGING RULE: Current public company messaging should lead with OWNER PROBLEM → STAKES → HOW DEALALITY HELPS. Do not lead with “marketplace” unless the specific product/use case being described genuinely requires it. Marketplace may be explained underneath the broader Dealality narrative where relevant. FUTURE STATE: This lock does NOT limit Dealality from building a substantial global marketplace/ecosystem over time. Strategic hierarchy: DEALALITY → broader platform; MARKETPLACE → important underlying platform / ecosystem layer — not DEALALITY = MARKETPLACE ONLY.
