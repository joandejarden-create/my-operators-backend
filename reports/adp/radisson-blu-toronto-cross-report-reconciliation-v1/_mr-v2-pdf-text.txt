Radisson Blu Toronto Downtown's most important issue is concentrated competitive displacement by Bisha Hotel, not a vague
visibility problem.
What the Data Is Telling Us
Bisha Hotel appears in 24 monitored scenarios where Radisson Blu Toronto Downtown is absent. That concentration matters more
than a long competitor list because it identifies a repeatable substitution pattern. Related Reality Gap pressure includes Outdoor
Event Space at 0% recognition, which may help explain why the hotel is not carried into those answers. Softest Scenario Capture is
Celebration at 20.0%. Bisha Hotel most often appears when the hotel is missing (24 scenarios).
Why It Matters
Leadership needs to understand why specific alternatives are surfaced instead of Radisson Blu Toronto Downtown in recurring
demand contexts. Otherwise attention may stay on average scores while missing the concentrated leak. A displacement pattern that
repeats is more actionable than a soft overall visibility narrative.
Where the Pattern Is Concentrated
Celebration is the softest Scenario Capture at 20.0%. Focus where the gap is concentrated rather than treating every territory equally.
Bisha Hotel appears most often when the hotel is missing (24 scenarios) — use that pattern for source and fact checks, not as proof
of cause. Reality Coverage is 19.0% (4 of 21 attributes). Start with gaps that matter operationally — beginning with Family-Friendly.
What Management Should Focus On Now
Focus first on the demand contexts where Bisha Hotel is consistently surfaced instead of Radisson Blu Toronto Downtown. Determine
whether the relevant attributes and use cases are clearly represented across priority sources, and whether the hotel's own
proposition is complete for those same contexts.
