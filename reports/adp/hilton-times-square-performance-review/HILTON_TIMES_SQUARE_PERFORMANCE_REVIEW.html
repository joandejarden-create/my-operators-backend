Hilton New York Times Square's primary issue is entering AI consideration at all, especially for Meetings & Groups.

AI Consideration is 23.1%, so the hotel appears in only a limited share of individual comparable responses. Rank tactics have limited value until relevance improves. The primary constraint is becoming part of the answer set for the traveler needs the hotel currently misses. Softest Scenario Capture is Group / Meeting at 15.4%. New York Marriott Marquis most often appears when the hotel is missing (29 scenarios).

Why It Matters

Fine-tuning rank against individual competitors will have limited value until the hotel becomes relevant in more of the traveler needs it currently misses. Entry problems and rank problems require different management attention. If leadership optimizes competitor comparisons first, the hotel can look busy while still missing the larger answer-set gap.

Reality Coverage is 50.0% (4 of 8 attributes). Start with gaps that matter operationally — beginning with Pet-Friendly.

What Management Should Focus On Now
