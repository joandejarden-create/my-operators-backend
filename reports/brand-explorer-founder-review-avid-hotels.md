## Brand positioning

avid hotels is a essentials-focused midscale / select-service IHG brand within IHG Hotels & Resorts. For owners, the affiliation case is efficient prototype economics, operating simplicity, guest essentials delivery, cost discipline, and IHG distribution. It should be evaluated on its own guest promise and operating implications, not as a generic parent-brand substitute or an interchangeable peer among Holiday Inn Express, EVEN Hotels, Garner hotels.

## Owner fit

## Where This Brand Creates the Most Value

### Scenario 1: Essentials new-build

A new-build midscale site where owners prioritize construction efficiency, lean staffing, and consistent guest essentials. avid is worth evaluating when the market supports value-led demand and the owner wants IHG distribution without Holiday Inn Express’s broader product stack. Owners should diligence whether avid hotels is a better fit than Holiday Inn Express for the specific asset, capital plan, and operator capacity.
