## Why Remington was not selected

Remington offers institutional US scale and appeared in Deal B production shadows, but selection is gap-driven:

1. Highgate already covers institutional 3P + CALA resort/lifestyle with stronger documented CALA inventory.
2. Driftwood better closes **conversion / turnaround** than another US-scale generalist.
3. Santa Fe better closes **Mexico resort + third-party** regional model.
4. Atlantica better closes **Southern Cone / Brazil multi-brand** adjacency for Deal B diagnosis.

Remington remains the **Wave 3** default institutional fill.
