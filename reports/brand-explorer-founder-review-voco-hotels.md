## Brand positioning

voco is a premium conversion-oriented soft brand within IHG within IHG Hotels & Resorts. For owners, the affiliation case is conversion and repositioning fit, retained property character, IHG platform access, standards and flexibility diligence, and market positioning versus boutique or soft-collection alternatives. It should be evaluated on its own guest promise and operating implications, not as a generic parent-brand substitute or an interchangeable peer among Hotel Indigo, Kimpton, Vignette Collection, Holiday Inn.
