PDAC 2027 Convention

What is happening

PDAC 2027 Convention (7–10 March 2027, MTCC) publishes Radisson Blu Toronto Downtown as an official hotel partner with promo rate CAD $449 and reference code for direct booking (cutoff 2 Feb 2027). Limited availability remains on select nights. PDAC does not use 3rd party housing bureaus; book directly with hotels

Fill remaining partner-rate inventory; pursue exhibitor/company suites and overflow once partner nights sell; do not confuse with exclusive venue housing.

Why this hotel

157 guestrooms / ~11,000 sq ft / 20 meeting rooms on Queens Quay — realistic for mid-size groups, overflow, and meeting+rooms packages; not exclusive MTCC mega-housing. PDAC official partner list includes this hotel at 8-minute walk to MTCC.

What is happening

NIST NICE 18th Annual Conference and Expo, June 7–9, 2027; location TBA.

Sources (1)
  • FIU NICE conference page

Potential business for this hotel

Potential hotel business depends on verified peak rooms, meeting agenda, and buyer authority for NIST / National Initiative for Cybersecurity Education (NICE). Public research has not confirmed a contracted room block at Bethesda Marriott. FIU / New America operate conference logistics under NIST award

Why this hotel

Bethesda Marriott inventory (407 guestrooms) and ~25,000 sq ft meeting space is unverified as a host until the NIST / National Initiative for Cybersecurity Education (NICE) venue/RFP path is confirmed. Do not treat an unannounced venue as hotel suitability.

Why act now

CONTACT NOW: Official NIST page still lists Location to be announced for June 7–9, 2027. Historical attendance (~400–450) is mid-size and consistent with Bethesda Marriott meeting inventory. Lead time ~9 months.

What is happening

2027 Ontario Water & Wastewater Conference & Trade Show is committed at Hotel X Toronto; Sheraton Centre Toronto; Hyatt Regency Toronto; Le Méridien Toronto Pinnacle Hotel (May 10, 2027 – May 13, 2027). The principal housing is not available to Radisson Blu Toronto Downtown. Venue is Enercare Centre, Exhibition Place. Hotel reservations open October 20, 2026 (announced opening; past as of mid-November 2026 research — do not treat as a future deadline)

Sources (3)
  • Ontarios Water Wastewater Conference Exposition 2027
  • Board And Staff
  • 2027 Joint Conference Present Partner And Connect

Potential business for this hotel

Future-cycle account development and/or separately evidenced overflow only — not the committed principal housing.

Why this hotel

Conditional supplementary delegate/speaker/exhibitor or executive lodging; no published room volume or overflow requirement. Radisson Blu Toronto Downtown cannot realistically win the committed 2027 principal housing; Convention-scale event (~2000 attendees); realistic role for a 157-room Harbourfront hotel is delegate/speaker overflow, VIP lodging and side functions — not primary housing.
