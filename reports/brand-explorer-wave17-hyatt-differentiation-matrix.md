## Lifestyle cluster: Thompson vs Dream vs Centric vs Caption

- **Hyatt Centric**: urban modern-explorer full-service lifestyle; exploration-forward; not nightlife-led.
- **Thompson Hotels**: design-led lifestyle with elevated F&B/social; cultural urban positioning.
- **Dream Hotels**: social/nightlife-leaning lifestyle identity; entertainment intensity higher than Thompson/Centric.
- **Caption by Hyatt**: select-service lifestyle with social commons / mixed-use development logic; not Centric full-service and not Hyatt Place conventional select-service.
