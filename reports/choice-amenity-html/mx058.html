      An affordable stay in Piedras Negras, Mexico

      Whether you’re traveling for business or leisure, alone or with family, our Quality Inn®Piedras Negras hotel near the Piedras Negras International Airport will help you get more for your money.       Start your day with free coffee and our free full hot breakfast. In-room desks are also available. When it’s time to relax, take a plunge in our large outdoor pool.

      Regardless of the reason for your trip, our Quality Inn will make you feel right at home. We look forward to accommodating your lodging needs on your next visit to Piedras Negras.

      Discover the charms of Piedras Negras

      With a convenient location in the heart of downtown Piedras Negras, our Quality Inn hotel makes a great base for exploring local sites, activities and attractions. The charming town of Piedras Negras is full of history, shopping, museums and restaurants featuring delicious local cuisine. Many festivals and events are hosted throughout the year. The city's main celebration is the La Feria del Sol (Festival of the Sun), which is held during the second week of July and features industrial, commercial, agricultural and cattle exhibitions. Don’t leave town without experiencing the captivating Spanish colonial ruins at the San Bernardo Mission, just 32 kilometers from the hotel.
