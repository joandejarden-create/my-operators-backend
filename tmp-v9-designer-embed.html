The Problem

Most Owners Do Not Lack Options.
They Lack a Fair Way to Compare Them.

Selecting a brand or operator is one of the most consequential decisions an owner makes — yet most owners still evaluate pitches in email threads, slide decks, and calls that were never designed to be compared apples to apples.

For Owners
Incomparable Pitches.
No Shared Framework.
Owners meet multiple brands, repeat the same story, and receive proposals in different formats — with little visibility into fit, economics, or what “normal” looks like for their asset and market.
Result: High-stakes decisions made on partial information and momentum, not clarity.
For Brands & Operators
Misaligned Outreach.
Misqualified Leads.
Development teams spend significant time responding to poorly scoped opportunities before the owner has done the work to be a serious counterparty.
Result: Wasted pipeline capacity and conversations that should never have started.
For Advisors & Partners
Looped in Late.
Limited Context.
Lenders, attorneys, and consultants typically enter after key conversations have already taken place. Deal structure is partially set and fragmented information is spread across emails, PDFs, and informal discussions.
Result: Advice on a process that’s already half-decided in someone else’s inbox.
