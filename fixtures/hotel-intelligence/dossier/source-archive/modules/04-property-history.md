## PROPERTY HISTORY TIMELINE — Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta

### Key Facts

- **Address:** Av. de las Garzas 136, Zona Hotelera, Puerto Vallarta, Jalisco, Mexico 48333

- **Year Built:** 2012

- **Year Last Renovated:** 2018

- **Rooms:** 451 (259 in Resort section + 192 in Hacienda adults-only section)

- **Floors:** 4

- **Category:** Grand Tourism (GSF classification), Superior First Class (Travel Weekly)

- **Model:** All-inclusive
