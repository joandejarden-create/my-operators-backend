## BRAND & REFLAG CHRONOLOGY — Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta

### RESOLVED: Breathless Puerto Vallarta IS the same asset as Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta

**Key Finding:** The Hyatt Breathless Puerto Vallarta Resort & Spa (announced Feb 14, 2024) is a **conversion/rebranding** of the existing Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta at Av. de las Garzas 136 — NOT a separate new-build project.

### 2Q25 Status Update (as of June 30, 2025 — filed July 24, 2025)

- **Hotel still listed as "Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta"** — 100% ownership, 451 rooms, Resort, Grand Tourism, in GSF's 2Q25 portfolio table (filed July 24, 2025).

- **No Breathless mention in 2Q25 report:** Francisco Zinser's Executive VP commentary in the 2Q25 report makes no reference to the Breathless conversion — the commentary focuses on occupancy records and RevPAR growth.

- **2025 Annual Report (filed ~Apr 2026):** Still refers to the asset as "Hotel Hilton Vallarta" in the related-party transactions section (p. 110-111), dated Dec 2025. This suggests the conversion may have been delayed significantly.

- **GSF total portfolio:** 25 properties (6,215 rooms) as of 2Q25, down from 26 (6,258 rooms) due to removal of SLS Cancun.

### Census Breathless Listing Assessment

- **CONTESTED → NOW SUPPORTED:** The Census affiliation listing Breathless Resorts & Spas is now understood as reflecting the ANNOUNCED conversion — the hotel was to become a Breathless-branded property. However, as of the latest public filing (2Q25, July 2025), the hotel was still operating as Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta.

- **CURRENT brand status:** Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta — the hotel continues to operate under this name. The original Q2 2025 Breathless conversion target has likely been delayed. Monitor GSF 3Q25, 4Q25, and 1Q26 reports for status updates.

### Current Status Confirmation (September 2026)

- **CONFIRMED: Hotel still operating as Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta.** TripAdvisor reviews posted Sep 1-3, 2026 reference "Grand Crystal" and "Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta." Google Maps lists the hotel as "Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta" — 5-star, actively bookable, 7,533 reviews (4.1★).

- **Hyatt Breathless page:** The Hyatt Inclusive Collection Breathless Mexico destinations page (hyattinclusivecollection.com/en/resorts-hotels/breathless/destinations/mexico/) does not list Puerto Vallarta among active Breathless locations. Currently active Breathless resorts are in Cancun (Riviera Cancun, Soul Cancun) and Cabo San Lucas.

- **GM Identified:** A TripAdvisor owner response from Feb 2025 is signed by "Luis A, Director General" — strongly suggesting the General Manager is Luis A. (possibly Luis Alberto or similar). This is partial — surname not yet confirmed.

### Conversion Status Assessment

- **Original timeline:** Announced Feb 14, 2024; target opening Q2 2025 (April 2025 per Hyatt Previews 2025 Resort Openings)

- **As of Sep 2026:** Over 17 months past original target with no public evidence of conversion. The hotel continues to operate under Krystal Grand branding with no evidence of dual-branding, renovation, or Breathless signage.

- **GSF silence:** No mentions of Breathless in GSF 1Q25, 2Q25, or 3Q25 quarterly reports. Francisco Zinser's last public mention was in the 4Q23 (Feb 2024) report.

- **Hyatt silence:** Hyatt's 2025 resort preview mentioned the opening but no subsequent update. The Breathless Puerto Vallarta page on hyatt.com is not active for reservations.

- **Assessment:** The conversion appears either significantly delayed or possibly cancelled. Commercial implications: if conversion is on hold, GSF may be open to alternative brand partnerships; if delayed, timing intelligence is critical for outreach.
