### Business Model

GSF operates a dual model: (1) owns and operates its own hotels, consolidating full P&L; (2) manages third-party-owned hotels, earning management fees only. The company describes its operating model as "multifunctional, efficient base and strict expense control." GSF focuses on acquiring, converting, developing, and operating hotels in the 4-star, 5-star, and Grand Tourism categories under all-inclusive and European plans. Also operates a Vacation Club for occupancy balancing.
