## OPERATOR & MANAGEMENT — Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta

### GSF Relationship: OWNER-OPERATOR (100% owned + self-operated)

**Key Finding:** Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta is both 100% owned AND operated by Grupo Hotelero Santa Fe. It is NOT a third-party management contract — GSF owns the asset and operates it under its own proprietary Krystal Grand brand.

[4][1][5]
### Relationship Type: Ownership + Direct Operation (Krystal Grand brand)

- **Owner:** Grupo Hotelero Santa Fe, S.A.B. de C.V. (100%)

- **Operator:** Grupo Hotelero Santa Fe (self-operated under proprietary Krystal Grand® brand)

- **Brand:** Krystal Grand® (GSF proprietary brand), with the hotel also listed on Hyatt Inclusive Collection platform as an "Independent Property"

- **Relationship to Hyatt:** The hotel is part of the Hyatt Inclusive Collection as an independent property, bookable through Hyatt channels. This appears to be a distribution/marketing arrangement, not a franchise or management agreement. The announced Breathless conversion would change this to a franchise/collaboration relationship.

- **Franchise:** None currently. The Breathless conversion would add a Hyatt franchise/collaboration layer.

[3][2]
### Post-Breathless Conversion Operating Structure (ANNOUNCED, not yet effective)

- Upon conversion: GSF remains 100% owner of the asset

- Brand: Breathless Resorts & Spas® (Hyatt Inclusive Collection)

- Expected operating model: GSF will continue to operate the hotel; Hyatt provides brand, distribution, and loyalty program access

- The Hyatt announcement describes it as a "collaboration" with GSF — consistent with GSF's pattern: Hyatt franchise/collaboration agreements with GSF as owner-operator (same model as Hyatt Centric Leon, Hyatt Place Aguascalientes, Hyatt Regency Insurgentes)
