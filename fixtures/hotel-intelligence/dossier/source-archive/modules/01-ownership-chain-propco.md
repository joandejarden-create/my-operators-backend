### Critical Note: Initial Assumption Reversed

The initial research directive assumed Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta was a third-party-owned hotel managed by GSF. The 4Q24 and 1Q25 earnings reports definitively show this hotel is **100% owned by GSF** — it appears in the "Company-owned" segment, NOT the "Third-party hotels under management" segment. This is a major correction to the research premise.

### Important Findings from 2024/2023 BMV Annual Reports

#### Property Ownership History (Critical Additions)

- **Original Development:** The Hilton Puerto Vallarta hotel was developed jointly by GSF and Grupo Chartwell. Construction began July 2011, and the hotel opened October 2012 with 259 rooms. This was GSF's first ground-up development.

- **2014 GSF acquires 50% from Grupo Chartwell:** In August 2014, GSF agreed to acquire the remaining 50% of the Hilton Puerto Vallarta from Grupo Chartwell. After the IPO (Sept 2014), GSF owned 100% directly or indirectly.

- **2016 Hacienda expansion acquisition:** In October 2016, GSF acquired adjacent real estate (54 private-use hotel units and undeveloped land, approximately 15,100 sqm) for EUA$12.0 million (Ps. 224 million). An additional Ps. 296 million was invested, totaling Ps. 520 million. The Hacienda opened April 2018 with 192 suites.

- **2022 Brand change:** The hotel changed from Hilton to Krystal Altitude in October 2022, then subsequently to Krystal Grand.

- **2024 Brand change announcement:** The Feb 2024 announcement plans conversion to Breathless Puerto Vallarta Resort & Spa.

#### Important Distinction: Krystal Resort Puerto Vallarta is NOT GSF-Owned

The 530-room Krystal Resort Puerto Vallarta (Av. Francisco Medina Ascencio) is **owned by Grupo Chartwell** and only operated by GSF under a management contract. The 2024 Annual Report states: "Actualmente los hoteles Krystal Resort Cancun, Krystal Resort Puerto Vallarta y Krystal Resort Ixtapa pertenecen a Grupo Chartwell y son operados por GHSF y no consolidan en Estados Financieros." The operating contracts were signed Feb 2010 and renewed Feb 2020 through 2030. This is a separate asset from Krystal Grand Puerto Vallarta.
