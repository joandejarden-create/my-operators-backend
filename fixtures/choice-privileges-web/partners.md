# Choice Privileges — Partners
Source: https://www.choicehotels.com/choice-privileges/partners

## Earn & redeem partners (named on site)
- **Cruises** — 5 points per $1 on soon sailings; onboard deals.
- **Preferred Hotels & Resorts** — luxury redemption network.
- **Bluegreen Vacations** — vacation packages; bonus points or reward card offers.
- **Penn Gaming** — 20+ casino hotels/resorts; reward nights.
- **Westgate Resorts** — 20+ participating resorts; book/redeem nights.
- **Avis / Budget** — up to 35% off + 1,000 CP points per rental.
- **e-Rewards** — surveys for CP points.
- **Trackhouse racing** — VIP experiences via points bids.
- **GetYourGuide** — 10% off tours/activities for members.
- **Entertainment** — ticket savings (theme parks, sports, concerts).

## Member discounts (non-points)
- **AAA** — 10% off participating hotels.
- **AARP** — 10% off participating hotels.

## Status match
Elite status match from other hotel loyalty programs for CP Elite benefits (point bonuses, extended reward booking windows, exclusive offers).

## Bank transfers
Same as redeem page: Amex MR, Capital One, Wells Fargo, Citi ThankYou.
