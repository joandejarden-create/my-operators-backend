# Choice Privileges — Benefits
Source: https://www.choicehotels.com/choice-privileges/benefits

## Program headline
Every stay brings you closer to rewards that matter. Join Choice Privileges and unlock milestone rewards and more flexible ways to earn and redeem.

## Elite status (2025+ program)
- **Gold Elite in 5 nights** (or faster with Choice Privileges Mastercard spend on hotel nights).
- **Titanium** — new top tier; includes Titanium Travel Award.
- **Milestone rewards** — bonus points and gift cards for staying.
- **Return and earn** — 1,000 bonus points after 2nd and 3rd qualifying stays each year.
- **Member rates** — save 10% or more on stays; exclusive member rates and early perks.
- **Military** — service members can receive Gold Elite status (account military flag).

## Earn (summary)
- Up to **10 points per dollar** booking direct at **7,100+** properties worldwide.
- **Choice Privileges Mastercard** — up to **16x total points** on stays; automatic Gold unlock for cardholders.

## Redeem (summary)
- Reward nights, experiences, gift cards; stays from **8,000 points**.
- Purchase points; partner earn (resorts/luxury).
