# Choice Privileges — Earn Points
Source: https://www.choicehotels.com/choice-privileges/earn-points

## Core earn
- **Up to 10 points per $1** when booking direct at **7,100+** hotels worldwide.
- **Choice Privileges Mastercard**: **16x–22x total points** on stays at participating properties.

## Elite qualification
- Gold and higher in as few as **5 qualifying nights**.
- **Elite Qualifying Credits** from hotel stays + Mastercard everyday spend (see FAQ).
- Tiers: Gold 5n/10k · Platinum 15n/30k · Diamond 35n/70k · Titanium 55n/110k.

## Other earn paths
- Member-only promotions and seasonal offers (deals page).
- **Buy points** — stock up for stays, gift cards, experiences.
- **e-Rewards** surveys — points without travel.
- **Choice Privileges Cruises** — up to $100 onboard spending; 5x points on cruise booking.
- **Partners** — Bluegreen Vacations, Penn Gaming Hotels, Westgate Resorts.
