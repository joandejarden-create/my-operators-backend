Dear Comfort, Country Inn & Suites by Radisson, and Sleep Inn Owners and Operators,
Recently, many of you have consistently shared a key concern: Insurance has become one of your most challenging and unpredictable costs. Rising premiums, higher deductibles, reduced capacity, and more restrictive coverage terms are impacting owners across the industry.
You shared this challenge. We listened, and we’re acting.
In response to your feedback, we’re actively developing a new Choice Property & Casualty Insurance Program designed specifically for your brands. This would be a completely optional program – not a brand standard – intended to provide you with a tool to help manage insurance costs in a difficult market. Our goal is to leverage the collective strength of your brands to attract quality insurance carriers, pursue more competitive terms, and create a stable, long‑term solution.
We’re working on this with Lockton Affinity, an existing vendor that currently supports the Choice Cyber Insurance program. They’ve been administering insurance solutions for franchise systems for nearly 30 years and have an extensive underwriting team with deep institutional knowledge of franchise concepts.
Scale matters:
In today’s insurance environment, individual hotels have limited leverage. But by coming together, we can present a credible, well‑understood risk profile – one that carriers are more willing to engage with seriously.
To continue developing this program, we’re launching a pilot and need a minimum of 300 participating hotels. Hotels that choose to participate would need to commit to the program for at least one year, should the pilot move forward. This level of commitment is essential to generate meaningful pricing and coverage opportunities, attract and retain carrier interest, and establish a foundation that can grow over time and potentially benefit additional Choice brands.
Without sufficient participation, it will not be possible to secure carrier engagement or achieve the broader objectives of the program – which are to help you save money.
While final terms will depend on carrier negotiations and participation levels, a successful program may offer:
Improved, lower pricing and coverage consistency
Access to insurance solutions that are difficult to obtain in the open market
Assistance with insurer engagement and appropriate claims handling
Greater transparency and coordination in claims management and loss control efforts
Participating hotels will need to provide the Choice Risk Management team with detailed information regarding their current insurance programs, including copies of current insurance declarations pages, recent loss runs from your insurers, and current insurance premiums.
I recognize this is a significant request. However, accurate and complete data is critical to evaluating risk, engaging carriers, and structuring a program capable of delivering real value. Any information shared will be treated confidentially and used solely for the purpose of evaluating and developing this pilot. Data will be shared only with approved internal stakeholders and potential insurance partners, as necessary. Once we have the program developed and ready to roll out, we will need at least 300 hotels to elect to participate.
