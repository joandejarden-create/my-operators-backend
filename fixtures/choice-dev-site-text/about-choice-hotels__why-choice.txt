Since 1941, when we launched the first U.S. hotel chain, our franchisee focus has driven our decisions. Today, we’re proud to help our hotel owners maximize their return on investment and grow their businesses.
You may be in business for yourself, but with Choice Hotels you'll never be by yourself. We work shoulder to shoulder with our franchisees, where the real work of ownership happens.
Owners succeed when guests show up and keep coming. Our brands earn that loyalty through experiences guests remember, backed by a name they trust.
The Choice Hotels Owner Success System brings together every person, program, and tool that delivers real value on the ground, designed for the day-to-day realities of your business.
We know our franchisees have a wealth of information and insight. That’s why we have active owners associations and advisory councils that gather valuable input from our owners on our brands, prototypes, support and more. Our associations ensure operations remain open and transparent.
