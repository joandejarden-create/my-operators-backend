Exploring your investment options? Here are some of the most common questions our development team hears about investing.
Part of being a franchise includes franchise fees. While all franchise companies are different, the typical fees you can expect to see include:
Affiliation Fees (One-time): The initial fee that grants access to the brand and associated intellectual property.
Royalty fees (Monthly): Either a percentage based on gross room revenue or the number of rooms within the hotel.
Marketing and Reservation Fees (Monthly): Sometimes called a system fee, these funds are used to expand the business through marketing and/or maintaining technology infrastructure and are often calculated based on room revenue.
Other Fees (Monthly, quarterly or yearly): Additional brand programs or usage fees. Some may be optional, while others are required.
Each franchisee is provided with a Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) containing all relevant initial franchise fees and the current ongoing fees for which they will be responsible. Please contact our team [link to contact] with any questions regarding franchise fees.
