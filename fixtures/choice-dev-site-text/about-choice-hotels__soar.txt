Choice Hotels International is committed to achieving greater belonging in lodging ownership and has a long history of creating and supporting a diverse franchisee base.
For over 20 years, the SOAR program has been dedicated to creating more belonging in the hospitality industry by attracting, mentoring and educating entrepreneurs from underrepresented backgrounds on their path to hotel ownership. Since it began, Choice has awarded and financially supported over 370 franchise agreements.
Choice Hotels’ incentive programs help extend the opportunity of hotel ownership to qualified individuals from groups that are underrepresented in the hospitality industry. These incentives are available for a variety of hotel projects and are subject to eligibility and program terms and conditions.
