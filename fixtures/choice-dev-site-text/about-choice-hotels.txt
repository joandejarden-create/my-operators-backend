Testimonials
I think Choice is very intentional about owner profitability, more intentional than any of the other brands I've worked with. Because if your partner doesn't succeed, you don't succeed. And Choice does a great job of recognizing it, and not just acknowledging it, but then they offer tools for franchisees to be able to utilize it.
With a franchise-first focus and an industry-leading voluntary retention rate, Choice Hotels has been committed to providing its hotel owners with the support they need to succeed since it launched the United States’ first hotel chain more than 80 years ago. From pre-opening to grand opening and every day forward, Choice provides hotel owners with best-in-class resources to help them maximize the return on their investment.
