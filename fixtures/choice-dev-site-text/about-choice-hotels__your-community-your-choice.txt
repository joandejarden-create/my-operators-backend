Each year, we invite all Choice franchisees to request funds for a local non-profit that’s special to them. Our associates vote and fund the projects they believe are most deserving.
As an advocate of local businesses since our founding over 80 years ago, Choice Hotels has placed our franchisees at the center of everything we do — from driving their growth and success to supporting the community initiatives they care about most.
Every year, we invite Choice Hotels franchisees to nominate a local non-profit organization that they believe deserves extra support. Our associates then vote to select recipients for our grant program.
Winners of the
“Your Community, Your Choice”
exemplify our commitment to serving the communities where our franchisees grow and live.
For over 80 years,
Choice Hotels
has championed local businesses by always putting our franchisees first. We’ve supported them through challenges and growth opportunities, and we've backed the community causes they're passionate about.
Our annual U.S. grant program invites our franchisees to nominate local non-profits they care about. Our associates vote to award funding for projects that make a real difference. The
