Everhome Suites was intentionally designed with prominent extended stay developers and operations experts to provide a fresh yet functional take on midscale extended stay. Deliver the amenities longer-staying guests require, powered by the efficiencies developers need to help drive revenue and thrive.
Midscale extended-stay is one of the fastest-growing segments of the hotel industry.
Efficient, value-engineered prototype and smart operating model designed for the unique needs of long-staying guests.
Incremental revenue opportunities through upgraded services and amenities including Homebase Market, more frequent housekeeping, faster Wi-Fi speeds and pet fees.
Industry-leading performance support tailored to the extended stay segment to help increase the profit opportunity.
Work with professional third-party management companies who are experts in the extended stay model.
Streamlined construction process with ready-to-build prototype.
