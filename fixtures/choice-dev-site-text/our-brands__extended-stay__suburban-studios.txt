The Suburban Studios brand offers hotel owners the chance to transform their property into an extended stay hotel and join one of the most resilient and highest-performing segments in the hospitality industry. By providing an efficient turnkey design and operating model, Suburban Studios meets the needs of longer-term guests while helping hotel owners maximize the value of their investments.
Flexible conversion options for investors interested in transforming older, transient hotels into refreshed extended stay properties.
Kitchen-in-a-Box and Lobby-in-a-Box design packages that transform traditional rooms and public spaces into spaces optimized for extended stay guests.
Dedicated extended stay sales and operations teams intended to help owners drive ROI and reduce costs.
Transform nearly any hotel into extended stay via the efficient, turnkey design.
Refined brand standards support lean operations and help with high margins.
Focused extended stay support and resources minimize operational disruption and accelerate ramp-up.
