As a premier economy hotel franchise, Econo Lodge offers owner-operators a recognizable brand backed by Choice Hotels’ powerful reservation and revenue management solutions to help drive franchisees’ ROI. As a long-time leader in economy hospitality, Choice Hotels and Econo Lodge deliver all the efficiencies developers need with the affordable stays and amenities that attract travelers.
Located along major highways, near airports and tourist destinations.
Conversion and design flexibility tailored to each individual property.
Guest rooms feature a modern and clean design with timeless signature artwork that appeals to guests.
Leverage a streamlined operational model and easy-to-maintain spaces for increased ROI potential.
Robust support from Choice Hotels to help maximize reservations and revenue.
Best-in-class booking and management tools streamline administration.
