Radisson RED
Radisson RED redefines the ordinary with its playful approach. Offering informal and flexible services where everything is possible, fostering a social environment that encourages sharing and connection, and embracing bold, vibrant designs that ignite excitement sets our hotels apart. This approach keeps the experience fresh, providing RED guests with limitless possibilities to seamlessly shift between work and leisure, creating an atmosphere that's both engaging and versatile.

Brand Information
RED is an upscale and select-service experience in vibrant and urban locations. RED is designed with attitude. It’s fun and flexible where guests can share and connect.
4 hotels with a combined 606 rooms in operation and 5 under development in the Americas as of September 30, 2023.
Offering developers a stand-out experience to create eye-catching essentials to one-off statements.
Conveniently located in North and South America to immerse guests in the food, drink, and culture of the city.
Amazing everyday essentials to Instagrammable statements, RED delivers a stand-out experience.
RED is aimed for travelers who want control over their experience. RED injects new life into hotels through informal services where anything goes, a social scene that’s waiting to be shared and bold design that kick-starts the fun.
All-day communal open space that combines the soul of a bar and great food. A buzzing hangout for meeting-ups, chilling out or working remotely.
Access on-demand with 24/7 fitness facilities, RED food and beverage offerings, and more!
Revive with RED’s amenities - rain showers, RED-branded bathroom amenities, professional hairdryers, and magnifying mirrors.
Latest technology that keeps guests on the grid with lightning-fast complimentary hotel-wide WiFi, an interactive lobby digiwall, and communal furniture pieces and arrangements for work and socializing.
The Radisson brands, including Radisson Blu, Radisson, Radisson RED, Radisson Collection, Radisson Individuals, Park Plaza, Country Inn & Suites, Radisson Inn & Suites, and Park Inn by Radisson, are owned in the Americas regions by Choice Hotels. Outside of the Americas, the brands are owned by Radisson Hotel Group, an unaffiliated company headquartered in Belgium.
