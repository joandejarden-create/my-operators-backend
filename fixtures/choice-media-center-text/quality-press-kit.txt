Brand Information
Quality is the fastest growing midscale brand and one of the largest brands in the Choice Hotels system:
There are more than 1,800 Quality brand hotels open around the globe, with over 1,600 in the United States alone.
The Quality brand’s growth is fueled by conversions, which delivers owners a high return on investment.
The brand’s high awareness and strong brand equity enable it to be competitive on pricing.
Quality brand hotels offer the amenities, or “Value Qs,” that guests desire, including:
Q Bed: Fresh linens, fluffy pillows, and plush blankets.
Q Breakfast: Complementary hot, fresh, and healthy breakfast selections.
Q Shower: Bright and roomy showers with multi-setting showerheads.
Q Service: Professional, responsive, and friendly staff.
Q Essentials: Free Wi-Fi, newspaper, coffee and tea selections, and in-room refrigerator.
Quality Inn guests can participate in the award-winning Choice Privileges® loyalty rewards program, top-rated in USA Today's 10Best Readers’ Choice Awards and by U.S. News & World Report for three consecutive years. Membership is free and offers fast rewards, instant perks, and exclusive member rates when booking directly at choicehotels.com.
For more information on the Quality brand hotels, visit https://www.choicehotels.com/quality-inn
