Brand Information
The Sleep Inn brand is a proven performer with the lowest costs to build in the highly attractive midscale segment.
More than 550 Sleep Inn hotels are currently open or in the pipeline worldwide, with rapid expansion in the works.
The Sleep Inn brand’s evolved prototype features a smart design and an efficient footprint -- with guest rooms that maximize space but don’t sacrifice function.
The brand features a timeless prototype to accommodate guests’ evolving tastes and behaviors, to attract guests of today and tomorrow.
The Sleep Inn brand provides a reliable, simply stylish stay at an affordable price.
The Sleep Inn design features soothing colors, nature-inspired designs, and modern furnishings for a happy night’s rest.
Signature amenities include:
ZENSES® bath products infused with essential oils.
Dream Cup coffee & tea featuring our signature blend, BE Rested®: Perfect Sleep tea.
White, nature-inspired bed linens.
Complimentary Morning Medley breakfast buffet, featuring a variety of hot and cold dining options.
Free Wi-Fi.
Swimming pools and/or fitness centers.
Dream tips throughout the guest experience, such as: “Rest fashionably – Wear your socks to bed for a better night’s rest.”
The Sleep Inn brand can also be found alongside MainStay Suites, an extended stay brand, as part of Choice’s innovative dual brand concept.
There are 10 Sleep Inn and MainStay dual-brand hotels open and more than 90 dual-brand projects in the pipeline as the concept rapidly expands throughout the U.S.
Sleep Inn guests can participate in the award-winning Choice Privileges® loyalty rewards program, top-rated in USA Today's 10Best Readers’ Choice Awards and by U.S. News & World Report for three consecutive years. Membership is free and offers fast rewards, instant perks, and exclusive member rates when booking directly at choicehotels.com.
For more information on Sleep Inn, visit www.choicehotels.com/sleep-inn.
