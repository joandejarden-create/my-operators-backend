Suburban Studios®
The Suburban Studios® brand from Choice Hotels is designed to offer guests a simple and enjoyable stay at a reasonable price. Franchisees have the opportunity to enter one of the fastest-growing segments of the hotel industry: extended stay.

Brand Information
Suburban Studios® hotels provide guests with the right amenities at the right price for longer-term stays, including:
Clean studio-style suites with spaces to eat, work, and relax.
Well-equipped kitchens.
On-site guest laundry.
Weekly housekeeping.
Free Wi-Fi.
Free Cable TV with premium movie channels.
There are more than 50 Suburban Studios® Hotel properties in the U.S. and Canada.
The cost-optimized new construction prototype allows owners the flexibility to make modifications to room counts, design, and amenities to compete effectively in their local markets.
The Suburban Studios® Hotel brand also offers conversion opportunities for existing extended stay brands, as well as select traditional hotel buildings, by featuring unique design guidelines for adding kitchen amenities to hotels with larger guest rooms.
Suburban Studios® Hotel guests can participate in the award-winning Choice Privileges® loyalty rewards program, top-rated in USA Today's 10Best Readers’ Choice Awards and by U.S. News & World Report for three consecutive years. Membership is free and offers fast rewards, instant perks, and exclusive member rates when booking directly at choicehotels.com.
For more information about Suburban Studios® hotels, please visit choicehotels.com/suburban.
