Brand Information
The MainStay Suites brand offers entry into one of the fastest growing segments of the hotel industry: extended stay.
There are more than 60 MainStay Suites hotels open in the U.S. and Canada.
MainStay Suites is a new construction-focused brand. The brand’s new prototype features enhanced interior and exterior community spaces, as well as a versatile suite layout.
MainStay Suites properties provide the following amenities:
Spacious and welcoming residential-style suites.
Fully-equipped kitchens in every room.
Uniquely designed Like Home programs that include a lending library and communal white board.
Complimentary continental breakfast.
High-speed internet access.
Weekly housekeeping services.
Laundry facilities.
Cable TV, including premium movie channels.
MainStay Suites can also be found alongside Sleep Inn as part of Choice’s innovative dual brand concept.
There are 10 Sleep Inn and MainStay dual-brand hotels open and more than 90 dual-brand projects in the pipeline as the concept rapidly expands throughout the U.S.
Guests of MainStay Suites properties can participate in the award-winning Choice Privileges® loyalty rewards program, rated no. 1 in USA Today’s 10 Best Readers’ Choice Awards list. Membership is free, offers fast rewards, instant perks, and exclusive member rates when booking directly at www.choicehotels.com.
For more information about MainStay Suites, please visit choicehotels.com/mainstay.
