Brand Information
Cambria is designed for the modern traveler, offering guests a distinct experience with little indulgences designed to help them stay inspired while away from home. Cambria Hotels offer design-forward accommodations with locally inspired design and décor, reflecting the unique personality of the surrounding community.
Sophisticated guest rooms with design-forward fixtures, abundant lighting, and plush premium bedding
Rooftop bars and outdoor spaces
Immersive spa-inspired bathrooms with Bluetooth mirrors
Customizable, unique meeting and event spaces
State-of-the-art fitness centers
Onsite restaurants with menus that capture the local flavors, as well as to-go options
Bars that featured a curated selection of local crafts beers, signature cocktails and Cambria Estate Wines
24/7 marketplace available, stocked with everyday necessities
Cambria Contactless Concierge
Free Wi-Fi and other tech-friendly features
The Cambria Hotels brand offers developers an approach to upscale that makes sense, and the brand is growing faster than ever with 8 locations opened in 2022.
Choice uses market research and specific criteria when identifying the locations of Cambria hotels, including proximity to area corporate businesses, convention centers, entertainment, and city attractions.
The brand welcomes new construction, conversion and adaptive-reuse projects.
Cambria Hotels has been ranked as one of the “Top 10 Hotel Brands in the U.S.”*
Cambria Hotels was ranked #1 in the upscale segment in the J.D. Power 2023 North America Hotel Guest Satisfaction Index Study.
Cambria Hotels participates in Choice Privileges, the award-winning loyalty program where members earn points on eligible charges during their stay, which can be redeemed for reward nights at nearly 7,400 hotels across 22 brands located in 45 countries and territories worldwide. With the Choice Privileges Mastercard, members can earn more points faster, including on everyday purchases.
For more information on Cambria Hotels, visit choicehotels.com/cambria.
* Based on the most hotel construction projects in its U.S. pipeline by Hospitality Net in 2017.
Unless otherwise stated, all statistics are from Choice Hotels International internal data.
