Brand Information
Whether traveling for a weekday business trip or a weekend of sightseeing, guests are sure to find the ideal Radisson hotel.
100 hotels with a combined 20,605 rooms in operation in key urban, suburban, resort and airport locations in the Americas as of September 30, 2024.
Offers developers an opportunity to join an iconic brand with strong performance and awareness and benefit from Choice’s transformative loyalty and commercial platforms.
Designed for scalability with easy to convert designs and operational efficiency.
With a nod to our storied past, our hotels are rooted in American style and sensibility, reimagining classic hotel features with stylish and subtly nostalgic yet modern accents to curate an environment that feels intimately familiar.
Hotels offer convenient amenities including on-site restaurants and bars, modern fitness facilities, complimentary wi-fi and flexible event and meeting spaces.
The Radisson brands, including Radisson Blu, Radisson, Radisson RED, Radisson Collection, Radisson Individuals, Park Plaza, Country Inn & Suites, Radisson Inn & Suites, and Park Inn by Radisson, are owned in the Americas regions by Choice Hotels. Outside of the Americas, the brands are owned by Radisson Hotel Group, an unaffiliated company headquartered in Belgium.
