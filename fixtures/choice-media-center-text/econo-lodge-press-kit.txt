Brand Information
Econo Lodge is one of the most recognized brands in the economy segment.
There are more than 800 properties open in the United States.
The brand offers conversion flexibility tailored to specific properties.
Easy to find, easy to book, and easy on the wallet, Econo Lodge hotels provide the amenities guests need while sticking to their budget.
Guest rooms are clean and inviting and have conveniently located outlets throughout the room to easily plug in devices.
Rooms offer a premium selection of movie channels.
Guests can grab a quick bite with their free Easy Starts Continental breakfast.
Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel.
Many Econo Lodge properties across the nation offer pet-friendly locations.
Econo Lodge guests can participate in the award-winning Choice Privileges® loyalty rewards program, top-rated in USA Today's 10Best Readers’ Choice Awards and by U.S. News & World Report for three consecutive years. Membership is free and offers fast rewards, instant perks, and exclusive member rates when booking directly at choicehotels.com.
For more information on Econo Lodge, please visit choicehotels.com/econo-lodge.
