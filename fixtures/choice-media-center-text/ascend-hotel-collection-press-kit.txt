Ascend Hotel Collection®
The Ascend Collection®, an upscale brand from Choice Hotels, is the first global soft brand featuring boutique, historic, and resort hotels. Sometimes the best travel experiences can’t be found in a guidebook. Distinctive properties designed for travelers seeking an authentic sense of place, where every stay reflects the unique charter and spirit of the destination.

Brand Information
Joining the Ascend Collection enables independent hotel owners to maintain their local charm and operational independence while gaining access to Choice’s industry-leading technology, robust distribution channels, and award-winning loyalty program.
Ascend® properties are in more than 400 locations worldwide, including: Denmark, Finland, Ireland, Japan, Norway, Spain, Sweden, Turkey, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, Ecuador, Mexico and throughout the Caribbean. Hotels are a mix of historic restoration, adaptive re-use, and new construction properties.
The Ascend Collection combines exceptional service with rich local character to provide guests with a one-of-a-kind upscale experience.
Ascend properties are found in popular urban markets as well as in off-the-beaten path destinations and range from charming inns to larger resorts.
Ascend properties are thoughtfully furnished to reflect the brand's boutique, historic, and resort feel, and may include area-inspired artwork, historical pieces, and locally sourced furniture.
Area-inspired food and beverage offerings are available at many properties.
Knowledgeable staff offer expert insights into the local area.
Guests of Ascend Collection properties can participate in the award-winning Choice Privileges® loyalty rewards program, top-rated in USA Today's 10Best Readers’ Choice Awards and by U.S. News & World Report for three consecutive years. Membership is free and offers fast rewards, instant perks, and exclusive member rates when booking directly at choicehotels.com.
For more information on the Ascend Collection, visit choicehotels.com/ascend.
