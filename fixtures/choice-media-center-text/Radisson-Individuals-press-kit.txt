Brand Information
Radisson Individuals are upper upscale hotels located in renowned cities, airports and key business and leisure destinations.
15 hotels with a combined 1,732 rooms in operation across the Americas as of September 30, 2024.
A young, dynamic brand conquering new frontiers. With strong brand awareness and an impressive growth trajectory, Radisson Individuals offers developers the chance to maintain and promote their uniqueness while benefiting from the experience and infrastructure of Choice.
Each property is hand-selected for its bold vision and commitment to exceptional service, standing against rinse-and-repeat experiences.
Radisson Individuals embody three main pillars for an exceptional guest experience: Characterful Encounters: Rooted in the thrill of exploration, we curate experiences that immerse guests in the local culture and spirit of each destination. Vivid Settings: Embracing the essence of each destination with bold design. Explorer’s Compass: Dedicated to delivering reliable service, our team embodies the mindset of an intrepid cartographer, facilitating to unlock curiosity through exploration and opening the door to new stories
The Radisson brands, including Radisson Blu, Radisson, Radisson RED, Radisson Collection, Radisson Individuals, Park Plaza, Country Inn & Suites, Radisson Inn & Suites, and Park Inn by Radisson, are owned in the Americas regions by Choice Hotels. Outside of the Americas, the brands are owned by Radisson Hotel Group, an unaffiliated company headquartered in Belgium.
