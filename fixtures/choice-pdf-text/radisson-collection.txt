We are thrilled to present to you the Brand Book for Radisson Collection. Beginning with the launch 
of the Radisson Hotel Group (RHG) in March 2018, we have developed these brand books to help 
clarify the connection each of our brands has to RHG and our overarching service culture, as well 
as highlight each brand�s unique attributes and activations. 
Every Moment Matters is about how we do business at the Radisson Hotel Group and who we are at the core � a place of 
purposeful encounters. 
For everyone. Everyday. Everywhere. Every time. 
At the same time, each brand has its own DNA: unique brand pillars, visual identity and value proposition.
Our vision is to become a top three hospitality company in the world. Our five-year operating plan includes initiatives that redefine 
our value proposition, optimize our portfolio, streamline operations, invest in new technology systems and align our team members 
to deliver on our signature brand promise: 
Every Moment Matters.
Across all seven brands, we have restructured our brand architecture and redefined our guest experience pillars: (1) Brilliant Basics 
(2) Memorable Moments (3) Local Experience (4) Feel at Ease. New design, products, programs and services that are referred toin
the Brand Book are at various stages of development, execution or implementation.
We hope this Brand Book supports and inspires you to help us bringing Radisson Collection to life for our guests on a daily basis.
