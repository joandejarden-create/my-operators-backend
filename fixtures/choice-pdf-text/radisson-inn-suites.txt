Radisson Inn & Suites Brand Platform 
 
Positioning  Upper-midscale, select-service hotels located in metropolitan areas, 
airport locations and mixed development communities. 
 
Essence  Inviting Real Connections. 
 
Audience  Travelers who mix business and pleasure. They seek convenience, 
comfort and are interested in sharing real connections with staff and 
fellow travelers thus connecting with the destination. 
 
Value Proposition  Radisson Inn & Suites delivers a heartfelt experience through inviting 
design, products and services ensuring guests feel welcome, valued and 
looked after. 
 
Brand Pillars  Naturally Grounded 
 Community Centered 
 Heartfelt hospitality 
 
Selling Line  Modern Comfort 
 
 
� Radisson Inn & Suites is an upper-midscale hotel brand designed to deliver heartfelt 
hospitality in a modern setting.  
 
� When guests visit a Radisson Inn & Suites hotel, they feel connected to the destination, and 
experience the best of local food, culture and people, which facilitates memorable moments 
(and lasting memories).  
 
� The hotels offer an inviting experience with comforts and conveniences both business and 
leisure travellers seek.

� We reworked and reinterpreted the architectural aesthetics, to bring the look up-to-date and

� Post pandemic design has put an important emphasis to provide guests access to exterior

outdoor experience provided.
