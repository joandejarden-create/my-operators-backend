1. Purpose of this Privacy Policy

When you share information with us, we can operate and improve the Services—including matching, workflows, market intelligence, and communication features. We want you to clearly understand how we use your information and how you can protect your privacy.

This Privacy Policy is designed to clarify:

  • What information we collect and why we collect it
  • How we use that information
  • The choices you have to access, share, and update information

As much as possible, we try to communicate in plain English. Terms such as "cookies," "browsers," and "IP addresses" have their ordinary meanings in online services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in several ways, including:

a) Personal Information

When you sign up for our Services and an account is created (your "Account"), we may ask for personal information, including but not limited to your name, email address, telephone number, company name, title, and related business contact details ("Personal Information"). If you take full advantage of the Services, you may also create a company or personal profile. By creating an Account and providing Personal Information, you allow others—including us and other members with whom you interact—to identify you, and you may not be anonymous.

Some features allow you to provide content to the Services (for example, deal submissions, operator or brand profiles, messages, documents, comments, or form responses). Content you submit may be retained by us as described in this Policy and our Terms of Service, including after you stop using certain features, subject to deletion requests where applicable.

b) Deal and Commercial Information

  • The sharing of Confidential Deal Materials (defined below) generally occurs when a member chooses to release those materials, or when a duly authorized representative authorizes Dealality to share materials on the member's behalf in connection with Platform workflows.
  • Information that reveals the identity of a company, property, owner, brand, operator, or project exploring a hotel or hospitality transaction is collected and stored primarily to facilitate Platform matching, evaluation, workflow, and related member-initiated processes.
  • We do not use identifying information in Confidential Deal Materials for unrelated sales, marketing, product development, model training, or publication purposes, except as described in this Policy (including limited anonymized or aggregated uses) or as authorized by you.

c) Member-to-Member Communications

Dealality may facilitate member-to-member communication via in-Platform messaging, email, or related tools. The content of those communications is intended for the directed participants. Dealality may access such communications where reasonably necessary to operate the Services, provide support, enforce our Terms, prevent fraud or misuse, or comply with law, as described in this Policy.

d) Commercial Acceptance and Billing Records

We may maintain records of your acceptance of our Terms of Service, Commercial Terms Schedules, Membership Agreements, discounts, founding participation status, and related commercial terms. Payment card or similar payment method details, if collected, are typically processed by a third-party payment processor and governed by that processor's terms and privacy policy.

Where required, we process Personal Information on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, and improve the Platform; prevent fraud and misuse; analyze Aggregate Information; and communicate about Service updates (balanced against your rights)
  • Consent — where we ask for it (for example, certain cookies, optional marketing, or optional product feedback), which you may withdraw at any time without affecting prior processing

We do not generally seek to process special-category (sensitive) personal data. Please do not submit such data unless we expressly request it and identify a lawful basis.

6. AI-Assisted Processing & EU AI Act Transparency

Important: Portions of the Platform may use artificial intelligence or machine learning systems to generate or assist with summaries, categorizations, match scores, recommendations, market intelligence, and similar outputs. Where the EU AI Act or similar laws apply, this section is intended to provide required transparency about those systems.

  • Nature of the AI: AI features are assistive decision-support tools. They do not, by themselves, make final binding decisions about membership eligibility, fees owed, creditworthiness, employment, or the legal or commercial terms of a hotel transaction.
  • Human oversight: You (and Dealality personnel, where applicable) remain responsible for reviewing AI-assisted outputs before relying on them. Do not treat AI outputs as professional advice or as a substitute for your own diligence.
  • Accuracy limitations: AI-assisted outputs may be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or inappropriate for your use case and are provided for informational support only.
  • Data used: We may process Personal Information and Platform content with AI tools and service providers to operate and improve those features, subject to this Policy and our Terms of Service.
  • No unrelated model training on confidential deals: We do not use Confidential Deal Materials that identify a specific deal, company, or person to train public generative AI models for unrelated marketing or publication, except where data has been anonymized or aggregated as described in this Policy, or where you have authorized a specific use in writing.
  • Providers: Third-party AI providers that process data on our behalf are expected to do so under contractual instructions and appropriate confidentiality and security measures, to the extent reasonably available.
  • Automated decision-making: We do not use solely automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you without meaningful human involvement. If that changes, we will update this Policy and provide any additional notices required by law.

15. Confidential Deal Materials

These provisions apply specifically to Confidential Deal Materials uploaded to or processed through the Platform.

a) Definitions

  • Confidential Deal Materials include, without limitation, deal teasers, information memoranda, financial statements, presentations, underwriting packages, operator or brand materials, supporting documents, and other materials related to a hotel or hospitality transaction or opportunity.
  • Anonymized Data means data stripped of personal and deal-specific identifiers such that neither Dealality nor a third party can reasonably re-identify the original person, company, or deal.

b) Limitations on Access

Access to Confidential Deal Materials is limited to personnel and systems reasonably required to operate Platform workflows, support members, enforce agreements, and comply with law. Personnel should not access such materials outside that operational scope.

c) Data Use and Anonymization

  • Dealality may use anonymized or aggregated data to improve Platform performance, produce general insights, or develop product features.
  • Identifiable deal-specific data will not be used for unrelated marketing or public publication.
  • Anonymized data is intended to be non-identifiable; no anonymization process is guaranteed to be irreversible in all circumstances.

d) Retention and Deletion

Upon deal closure, abandonment, or written request, users may instruct Dealality to delete related Confidential Deal Materials by emailing hello@aohospitalityadvisors.com, subject to legal and operational retention needs and copies held by other members with whom materials were shared.

e) User Warranties

Users uploading or sharing deal materials represent that they have authority to do so and that sharing does not violate third-party agreements or applicable law.

f) Security Measures

Dealality uses access controls, authentication, and logging, and may use additional protections (such as watermarking or restricted sharing) where available, to help protect deal materials. Members remain responsible for how they download, forward, or store materials outside the Platform.
