The usual process can narrow the decision before it is fully understood.

Major hotel decisions are often shaped through existing relationships, individual presentations, scattered emails, and sequential conversations. Each input may be useful. Together, they rarely create a complete or comparable view of the opportunity.

  • Credible alternatives may never be explored.
  • The first available relationship may shape the opportunity too early.
  • Brand enthusiasm may be mistaken for strategic fit.
  • Proposals may rely on different assumptions.
  • Important commercial or contractual gaps may emerge late.
  • Sequential negotiation may weaken the owner’s leverage.

A stronger decision begins with more than one credible path.

Dealality turns one hotel opportunity into a confidential and structured selection process—so owners can understand the possibilities before committing to a particular relationship or structure.
