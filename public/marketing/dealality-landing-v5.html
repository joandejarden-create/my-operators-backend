The Problem

Most owners do not lack options.
They lack a structured way to understand them.

Selecting a hotel brand, operator, or franchise partner is one of the most consequential decisions an owner makes. The information environment and process around it have not kept pace with that importance.

For Owners
Incomplete Information.
No Structured Comparison.
Owners approach multiple brands simultaneously, repeat their story to each one, and receive responses in inconsistent formats — with no structured way to understand which options actually fit the asset, market, ownership goals, conversion economics, or long-term operating strategy.
Result: Major decisions made without the intelligence to support them.
For Brands & Operators
Misaligned Outreach.
Misqualified Leads.
Development teams spend significant time responding to poorly scoped opportunities. The cost is not just effort — it is missed alignment with owners who were the right fit but came through the wrong channel with the wrong information.
Result: Wasted pipeline capacity and conversations that should never have started.
For Advisors & Partners
Looped in Late.
Limited Context.
Lenders, attorneys, and consultants typically enter after key conversations have already taken place. By the time they are engaged, deal structure is partially set and fragmented information is spread across emails, PDFs, and informal discussions.
Result: Constrained influence and decisions already shaped without them.
What Dealality Is
Dealality is not a public marketplace. It is a private decision layer for hotel owners evaluating brands, operators, and deal partners — combining market intelligence, structured comparison, and confidential deal workflow in one purpose-built platform.

How It Works

From intelligence to intake
to LOI — privately.

Dealality structures the evaluation from initial intelligence gathering through to LOI. Final negotiations and legal steps remain between the parties and their professional advisors.

Step 01
Brand & Operator Intelligence
Owner accesses Brand Explorer, Operator Explorer, and Market Intelligence to understand which partners may fit and where readiness gaps exist — before anything is shared.
Step 02
Guided Deal Intake
Owner submits project details once through structured intake. Readiness summary identifies what to address before outreach begins.
Step 03
Criteria-Based Matching
Platform scores and aligns the project with brand and operator profiles. Only parties meeting the owner's stated criteria are surfaced.
Step 04
Owner Selects Who to Engage
Owner reviews the matched shortlist and selects which parties to invite. Selected parties respond with commercial terms in a standardized format.
Step 05
Structured Term Comparison
Owner compares offers side-by-side — fees, royalties, PIP scope, territory terms — and shortlists finalists before the first direct call.
Step 06
NDA-Gated Deal Room & Exit
Secure virtual room for finalists. NDAs executed digitally. Platform steps back at LOI — final negotiations proceed between the parties and their advisors.

Confidentiality is the foundation of how the platform works — not a feature added on top. All projects are private by default. Confidential materials such as PIP scopes, pro formas, and technical briefs are stored in NDA-gated deal rooms. NDAs are executed digitally through the platform before any confidential document is accessible. No information is shared across parties or used for any purpose other than the specific deal it was submitted for.
Dealality's role ends at LOI. Once the owner has selected a preferred brand or operator and the parties are ready to proceed, they move offline for MA/FA drafting, franchise agreement negotiation, and legal documentation. Dealality does not participate in those discussions and does not earn any ongoing commission from the transaction. Our success-based fee for owners applies at the LOI milestone.
Membership on the brand and operator side is by application and approval. We do not list every brand or operator — only those who are actively engaged in development and willing to participate in a structured, responsive process. Brands and operators complete a profile that includes their development criteria, geography, chain scale, fee parameters, and operational model. This profile is what owners see in Brand Explorer and Operator Explorer.
AI is used as a supporting capability — not the core product. Specifically, AI generates structured deal summaries from owner submissions, helping reduce preliminary qualification time for brands and operators. It is not used to make decisions, recommend brands, evaluate projects, or substitute for professional judgment. All matching, comparison, and selection decisions remain with the owner and the parties involved.
