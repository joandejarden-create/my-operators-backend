The Problem

Most owners do not lack options.
They lack a structured way to understand them.

Selecting a hotel brand, operator, or franchise partner is one of the most consequential decisions an owner makes. The information environment and the process around it have not kept pace with that importance.

For Owners
Incomplete Information.
No Structured Comparison.
Owners approach multiple brands simultaneously, repeat their story to each one, and receive responses in inconsistent formats — with no structured way to understand which options actually fit the asset, market, ownership goals, or long-term operating strategy.
Result: Major decisions made without the intelligence to support them.
For Brands & Operators
Misaligned Outreach.
Misqualified Leads.
Development teams spend significant time responding to poorly scoped opportunities. The cost is missed alignment with owners who were the right fit but came through the wrong channel with the wrong information.
Result: Wasted pipeline capacity and conversations that should never have started.
For Advisors & Partners
Looped in Late.
Limited Context.
Lenders, attorneys, and consultants typically enter after key conversations have already taken place. Deal structure is partially set and fragmented information is spread across emails, PDFs, and informal discussions.
Result: Constrained influence and decisions already shaped without them.
What Dealality Is
Dealality is not a public marketplace. It is a private decision layer for hotel owners evaluating brands, operators, and deal partners — combining market intelligence, structured comparison, and confidential deal workflow in one purpose-built platform.

How It Works

From intelligence to intake
to LOI — privately.

Dealality structures the evaluation from initial intelligence gathering through to LOI. Final negotiations and legal steps remain between the parties and their professional advisors.

Step 01
Brand & Operator Intelligence
Owner accesses Brand Explorer, Operator Explorer, and Market Intelligence to understand which partners may fit and where readiness gaps exist — before anything is shared.
Step 02
Guided Deal Intake
Owner submits project details once through structured intake. Readiness summary identifies what to address before outreach begins.
Step 03
Criteria-Based Matching
Platform scores and aligns the project with brand and operator profiles. Only parties meeting the owner's stated criteria are surfaced for consideration.
Step 04
Owner Selects Who to Engage
Owner reviews the shortlist and invites selected parties. Invited parties respond with commercial terms in a standardized format — no unstructured PDFs.
Step 05
Structured Term Comparison
Owner compares offers side-by-side — fees, royalties, PIP scope, territory terms — and shortlists finalists before the first direct call.
Step 06
NDA-Gated Deal Room & Exit
Secure virtual room for finalists. NDAs executed digitally. Platform steps back at LOI — final negotiations proceed between the parties and their advisors.
