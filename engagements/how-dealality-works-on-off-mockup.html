Proposed for How Dealality Works
How Dealality Works A Connected Process From Opportunity to Agreement.

Turn One Hotel Opportunity Into a Structured Decision Process.

Dealality organizes the opportunity on platform—and facilitates the offline conversations that actually close deals. People meet in person or on a call; Dealality keeps those moments prepared, tracked, and connected to the process.

People meet offline. Dealality stays with them. Introduction, diligence, and final alignment happen between people—prepared, facilitated, and tracked in the platform so nothing falls out of the process.

One Opportunity. One Connected Process.

From the first question to the selected direction, Dealality keeps the opportunity, participants, proposals, and decision criteria connected.

Journey shape (8 steps): 01 Define → 02 Explore → 03 Prepare & Engage → 04 Meet & Align → 05 Compare → 06 Collaborate & Review → 07 Pursue → 08 Align & Hand Off. Offline steps are first-class in the nav. Platform / Offline appear once as a legend; step type is shown by number color (blue vs gold), not labels beside each title.
