PUERTO VALLARTA

Embraced by the lush palm-covered Sierra Madre Mountains, Puerto Vallarta’s sandy beaches open onto the bright turquoise Pacific Ocean. Earning the honor of being voted one of Mexico’s most romantic places, this enticing coastal destination regularly tops vacation lists with its charming cobblestone streets, bright terracotta-roofed houses, and stunning secluded beaches. Friendly locals welcome you to stylish restaurants and quirky shops throughout the city’s center, while rhythmic music pumps out of busy beachside bars and clubs into the night air along the malecon. Experience a bucket-list worthy flight on the longest and fastest zipline in Mexico or explore a picturesque underground beach on the Marieta Islands. Whether on foot, horseback or boat, the epic beauty of Puerto Vallarta is inescapable.
