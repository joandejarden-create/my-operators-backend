Welcome to

Caribbean Resort & Villas

We’ve been busy since your last visit! Our $20+ Million Dollar Renovation is COMPLETE and we can’t wait to welcome you back to an elevated level of luxury, style, comfort, and relaxation.

The Caribbean Resort has been the vacation choice of families and couples for over 40 years, voted the #1 Hotel for Families, Golf Packages, and Overall Hotel on the Grand Strand by the Sun News “Best of the Beach Awards.” We’ve taken the Myrtle Beach family experience to another level. Enjoy spacious and modern-styled accommodations.  

Our amenities include an outdoor water attraction, The Pirate’s Plunge, and a 200-foot long lazy river outside and even one inside too! The Caribbean also has a fitness room, game room, and the Mist Spa. With onsite food and beverage options such as Starbucks™, Ben & Jerry’s, an oceanfront tiki bar, and Sea Captain’s House, the whole family can enjoy a variety of meal options on vacation!
