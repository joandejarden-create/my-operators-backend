Vista Playa de Oro Manzanillo

SET SAIL FOR A
PIRATE WORLD

Vista Playa de Oro Manzanillo is an unforgettable family resort in Manzanillo, Colima, Mexico. Kids love our pirate-themed water park, while adults enjoy the picturesque setting with tropical gardens, colorful bougainvilleas,  cobblestone paths, lush greenery, white stucco facades, and terracotta rooftops.

You and your crew can spend the day across the street at beautiful Miramar Beach, get soaked aboard the giant pirate ship and water slides at Kraken Aquapark, savor delicious bites at our two restaurants, two snack and four bars, or just kick back and soak up the sun. There’s truly something for everyone to enjoy!
