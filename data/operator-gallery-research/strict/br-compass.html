Welcome To

Compass Cove Oceanfront Resort

We’ve been busy since your last visit! Our $25+ Million Dollar Renovation is COMPLETE and we can’t wait to welcome you back to an elevated level of luxury, style, comfort, and relaxation.

Conveniently located near the heart of Myrtle Beach, Compass Cove has been a family favorite for generations. With over 530 well-appointed one, two, and three-bedroom condominiums and an array of amenities, it’s easy to see why! Compass Cove features twenty-three exciting pool features including six indoor and outdoor pools, three indoor and outdoor lazy rivers, seven hot tubs, kiddie pools, and much more. At Compass Cove Resort, you will find a variety of suites and amenities that range from cozy and comfortable to fun-filled and exciting.
