Welcome To

Grande Cayman Resort

Relax to the roar of the ocean waves and feel the cooling hint of the saltwater breeze while lounging at our many pools, gliding along our lazy river, and racing down our waterslide. The Grande Cayman Resort, formerly Long Bay Resort, water amenities feature indoor and outdoor pools, the Silly Submarine water park with a waterslide, hot tubs, kiddie pools, and the 250-foot lazy river, which is enclosed and ideal for tube rides all year long. Grande Cayman Resort also features the best in food and beverage with a variety of restaurants and bars both on location and within walking distance including Starbucks, Due South, Turtle Bay Cafe, and a Tiki Bar.  Guests of Grande Cayman Resort also receive FREE tickets to Myrtle Beach’s favorite attractions through our Brittain Rewards program.
