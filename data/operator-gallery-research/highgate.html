The Leading Hospitality Investment & Management Company

Highgate is the industry-leading hotel management, investment, technology and development firm, with a diverse portfolio of hotels across North America, Europe, the Caribbean, and Latin America.

  • 30 Year Track record as an innovator in the hospitality industry. Across a 200+ person corporate team, our executives each carry decades of focused expertise
  • 79,348 Keys Over 400 properties comprising over 79,000 owned and/or managed hotel rooms
  • $15+ Billion Over $5B in total revenue under management, equating to an aggregate real estate value of over $15B

Businesses & Capabilities

  • Management Unparalleled reputation for operational optimization, with deep skill sets in revenue management & distribution, hotel branding and experiential curation
  • Development Extensive experience executing on ground-up development, adaptive re-use, redevelopment projects and hotel renovations
  • Investment Management Longstanding track record of delivering favorable returns to partners through operational outperformance and innovative investment strategies
  • Technology Ventures In-house venture capital investment platform focused on incubating innovative hospitality-linked technology
