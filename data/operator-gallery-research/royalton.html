FAQ

About Royalton Resorts  


Brand, Value, and Overall Experience

  • What is included in an all inclusive beachfront resort at Royalton Resorts?

Royalton Resorts offer a comprehensive all inclusive beachfront vacation experience designed for modern travelers. Each stay includes luxury accommodations, unlimited dining across multiple restaurants, premium beverages, daily activities, fitness programs, kids and teens clubs, non-motorized water sports, and professionally produced entertainment. Located in top Caribbean and Mexico beach destinations, everything is seamlessly included in one experience.

  • Are all inclusive resorts worth it?

All-inclusive resorts are worth it when they combine quality, variety, and convenience. Royalton Resorts deliver strong value by including accommodations, dining, drinks, activities, and entertainment in one price while maintaining a high standard of food, service, and beachfront experience expected from modern luxury resorts.

  • What makes Royalton Resorts different from other all inclusive resorts?

Royalton Resorts stand out by combining prime beachfront locations with a cohesive guest experience that integrates design, service, dining, and entertainment. Each resort follows a consistent brand philosophy, ensuring a modern, immersive, and recognizable all-inclusive experience across destinations.

  • What are the different Royalton Resorts brands?

Royalton Resorts includes a diverse portfolio: Royalton Luxury Resorts (family-friendly), Planet Hollywood Beach Resorts (entertainment-driven), Hideaway (adults only relaxation), CHIC (social adults only), Planet Hollywood Adult Scene, Vessence (wellness-focused), and Mystique (boutique beachfront). Each brand offers a distinct all inclusive experience.

  • Why choose Royalton Resorts for an all inclusive beach vacation?

Royalton Resorts combine modern beachfront luxury with a complete all inclusive concept, offering convenience, quality, and a cohesive vacation experience in top Caribbean and Mexico destinations.

Destinations & Locations  


Where you can travel

  • Where are Royalton Resorts located?

Royalton Resorts are located in some of the most popular beachfront destinations in the Caribbean and Mexico, including Cancun, Riviera Maya, Punta Cana, Montego Bay, Antigua, Saint Lucia, Grenada, Barbados, and Costa Rica, offering a wide variety of travel options.

  • Are Royalton Resorts located on the beach?

Yes, all Royalton Resorts are located on prime beachfront settings in destinations such as Cancun, Riviera Maya, Punta Cana, Montego Bay, Antigua, Saint Lucia, and Barbados. Many properties feature oceanfront suites, private beach areas, and swim-out rooms, allowing guests to stay just steps from the ocean.

  • How far are Royalton Resorts from the airport?

Most Royalton Resorts are conveniently located within 15 to 45 minutes from major international airports, making transfers efficient and accessible for travelers arriving in Caribbean and Mexico destinations.

  • When is the best time to visit Royalton Resorts?

Royalton Resorts offer year-round vacation experiences. The best time to visit depends on destination, weather preferences, and travel seasonality, but guests can expect warm climates and beachfront experiences throughout the year.

Accommodations & Upgrades  


Rooms, suites, and elevated experiences

  • What types of rooms and suites are available?

Royalton Resorts offer accommodations ranging from modern guest rooms to spacious suites, swim-out rooms, and family suites. Premium options include Chairman Suites, Presidential Suites, and overwater bungalows at select resorts, designed for guests seeking elevated comfort and space.

  • Are Royalton Resorts good for groups and large families?

Yes, Royalton Resorts are well suited for group travel, celebrations, and large families. Flexible room configurations, connecting suites, and shared spaces allow guests to stay together comfortably while enjoying a beachfront vacation experience.

  • Is Diamond Club or Star Class worth it?

Yes, these upgrades provide enhanced experiences including butler service, premium room locations, exclusive beach and lounge areas, and personalized service that elevates the overall stay.

  • What distinguishes the Royalton Suites & Villas collection?

The Royalton Suites & Villas collection represents the pinnacle of luxury at Royalton Resorts, featuring our most exclusive accommodations like Overwater Bungalows, Chairman Suites, and Presidential Suites. Guests staying in these categories enjoy top-tier inclusions, such as dedicated butler service and expanded Diamond Club™ amenities, designed for those seeking a more private and sophisticated vacation.

Experiences & Amenities  


Dining, entertainment, spa, fitness

  • Is the food and beverage experience high quality?

Yes, Royalton Resorts are known for offering elevated food and beverage experiences within the all inclusive category. Guests can choose from multiple restaurants featuring international cuisines, along with premium cocktails prepared by professional bartenders, delivering a dining experience comparable to standalone venues.

  • What entertainment is available at Royalton Resorts?

Royalton Resorts provide a wide range of entertainment options including live shows, music performances, themed events, and immersive evening programming. Entertainment is designed to complement the beachfront setting while creating engaging and memorable experiences throughout the stay.

  • Do Royalton Resorts offer spa and wellness experiences?

Yes, Royalton Resorts feature full-service spas with hydrotherapy circuits, treatment rooms, and wellness programs. Guests can enjoy massages, facials, and specialized therapies designed for relaxation and recovery.

  • Do Royalton Resorts have gyms and fitness programs?

Yes, Royalton Resorts offer modern fitness centers, Royalton Fit programs, group classes, and sports facilities. Guests can maintain or enhance their fitness routines while on vacation.

Travel Styles, Weddings & Loyalty  


Who it’s for + booking benefits

  • Are Royalton Resorts good for families?

Yes, Royalton Resorts are designed for family travel with experiences for all ages. Guests can enjoy kids clubs, teen lounges, family-friendly entertainment, and spacious accommodations such as family suites and connecting rooms. This allows families to travel together while each member enjoys personalized activities.

  • Do Royalton Resorts offer adults only options?

Yes, Royalton Resorts offers multiple adults-only experiences tailored to different travel styles. Hideaway focuses on relaxation and privacy, CHIC delivers vibrant social energy, and Planet Hollywood Adult Scene offers a celebrity-inspired adults-only atmosphere. All are designed exclusively for guests 18 and older.

  • Are Royalton Resorts good for destination weddings?

Yes, Royalton Resorts offer destination wedding experiences with beachfront venues, dedicated planning teams, and customizable packages. Weddings become multi-day celebrations where guests can enjoy a shared vacation alongside the event.

  • Can you earn Marriott Bonvoy points at Royalton Resorts?

Yes, guests staying at participating Royalton Resorts can earn Marriott Bonvoy points and Elite Night Credits, allowing them to accumulate rewards while enjoying an all inclusive beachfront vacation.

  • Are Royalton Resorts part of Marriott Bonvoy?

Yes, Royalton Resorts participates in the Marriott Bonvoy program, enabling guests to enjoy loyalty benefits, earn points, and access exclusive member advantages during their stay.

  • Why book Royalton Resorts directly?

Booking directly through official channels provides access to exclusive offers, the best available rates, and Marriott Bonvoy benefits, ensuring a more seamless and rewarding booking experience.
