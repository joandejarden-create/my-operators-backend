Including past and current hotels in the portfolio, Arbor Lodging has owned, operated, or financed hotels across 30 states in the United States. Our diverse portfolio reflects our expansive network of relationships that allows us to source and analyze compelling opportunities, as well as our strong foundation of best practices that allows us to scale our operational and strategic expertise over multiple markets. We are honored to have managed hotels under the high standards of some of the world’s most renowned brands, including Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, and IHG.
