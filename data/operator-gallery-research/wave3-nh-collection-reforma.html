      The NH Collection Mexico City Reforma hotel sits in the heart of the upscale Zona Rosa neighborhood, just a 2-minute walk from Mexico City's financial district. The tourist attractions in the historic center are only 15 minutes away on the subway and there are high-end shops, bars and restaurants on the doorstep.

      • Located in the exclusive Zona Rosa — Mexico City's iconic LGBTQ+ district, with high-end shops, bars and restaurants on the doorstep — NH Collection Mexico City Reforma is a Pride hotel where every guest is celebrated and welcomed
      • 2-minute walk to the financial district
      • 15-minute subway ride to the historic center

      Our 306 guest rooms are elegantly decorated in a classic style. All of them come with modern comforts such as TVs 48'' and free Wi-Fi. The rooms on the upper floors get spectacular views across the financial district's skyline.

      • 306 spacious, classic-style rooms
      • TVs 48'' and free Wi-Fi
      • Sweeping city views from the upper floors

      The hotel restaurant offers a huge choice of Mexican and international dishes. There's a stylish bar, too, plus a modern gym. Outside, our swimming pool takes center stage. Our expert Guest Relations team are also on hand to make reservations and to show you the city's hidden gems.

      • Restaurant with international and Mexican cuisine
      • Heated outdoor pool
      • Modern gym
      • If you're planning on staying with us over the weekend, book directly on nh-hotels.com/en, sign up to Minor DISCOVERY and take advantage of our "Lazy Sundays", which include a late checkout (before 3pm) free of charge.

      Explore our rooms

      There are 306 rooms at NH Collection Mexico City Reforma, all of which have been recently renovated to the highest standards. Well-lit, comfortable and classic, the rooms are equipped with essential amenities for the modern traveller, including high-speed Wi-Fi, large flat-screen televisions, and tea and coffee making facilities.

      Are meeting rooms available at the NH Collection Mexico City Reforma hotel?

      Yes. The hotel is able to host your meetings and conferences or large gatherings. 16 function rooms are available, the largest of which can accommodate 450 people.

      What are the main points of interest close to the NH Collection Mexico City Reforma hotel?

      The hotel sits in the heart of the Zona Rosa neighborhood, a two-minute walk away from the financial district and 15-minute subway ride away from Mexico City’s historic center.

      What are some of the property amenities at the NH Collection Mexico City Reforma hotel?

      The hotel offers classic-style rooms, some of which have spectacular views of the financial district’s skyline. A hotel restaurant offering Mexican and international dishes, a stylish bar, a modern gym and an outdoor pool are also available for guests.

      Does the NH Collection Mexico City Reforma hotel offer any food or drink options?

      Yes. The hotel restaurant offers a large variety of Mexican dishes and a bar is also available for morning coffee, after-dinner cocktails and fresh-made snacks.

      Does the NH Collection Mexico City Reforma hotel offer a parking service?

      Yes, you may park at the hotel for 12 USD per day.
