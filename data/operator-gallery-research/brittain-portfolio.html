Our Portfolio

From award-winning, full-service oceanfront resorts, to nationally recognized brands such as Marriott, to affordable and family-friendly hotels, Brittain Resorts & Hotels operates a broad range of properties. With over 4,700 rooms, suites, and condos and 45+ food and beverage outlets under management, we consistently ensure each and every property is performing at the highest level by leading with an entrepreneurial spirit, yet supported by proprietary systems and processes. By understanding the goals and desires of each client, coupled with a results-driven team with proven resources, Brittain Resorts & Hotels is able to deliver returns that would otherwise be unattainable.
