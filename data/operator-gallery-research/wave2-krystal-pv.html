    WELCOME TO Krystal Grand® Puerto Vallarta

    DISCOVER AN UNFORGETTABLE EXPERIENCE

    The perfect setting for your vacation in Puerto Vallarta, with one of the cleanest beaches in the world.

    We are just 10 minutes from Puerto Vallarta International Airport and the Cruise Terminal, and 15 minutes from the traditional Malecón with its art galleries and restaurants.

    Krystal Grand® Puerto Vallarta is an all-inclusive hotel with two sections: Resort and The Hacienda®, the first ideal for family enjoyment, and the second designed for a romantic couples’ getaway.

    Enjoy an unparalleled flavor at our seven restaurants, two snack bars, an adults-only sushi bar, two cafés and four bars.

    Have fun with our family-oriented dynamics and contests. We also have Kamp Krystal® for the little ones and Adventure Friends® for teenagers, where they’ll have an amazing time.

    Discover Krystal Grand® Puerto Vallarta and live an unforgettable experience!

    Discover The Hacienda®

    hacienda

    At The Hacienda®, every detail enhances your stay: suites with private terraces, personalized service, and exclusive benefits such as gourmet breakfast, access to specialty restaurants, and a private pool for adults only.

    Here, comfort and exclusivity combine with the magic of Banderas Bay on the Mexican Pacific.

    Hotel

    Resort

    Escape to paradise at our hotel with a prime beachfront location in Puerto Vallarta. You'll be just 15 minutes from the vibrant downtown area.

    Enjoy exquisite cuisine at three restaurants, including two specialty restaurants, a café, a snack bar, and an exclusive sushi bar for adults only. Relax in our two family pools or the serene adults-only pool.

    Spacious and comfortable rooms designed for unparalleled rest await you. Ask about our various stay plans and book your unique experience.

    Rooms

    Confort and relaxation

    The Resort section rooms are spacious and have spectacular views; whether panoramic ocean views, semi-partial ocean views, or views overlooking the resort. Some have a balcony or terrace. If you’re looking for more peace and quiet to enjoy some time with your partner, stay at The Hacienda in one of our 192 uniquely decorated suites that highlight the true essence of Mexico. Some of these suites have a private pool, terrace, or balcony. All our rooms have a minibar and 24/7 room service ($).
