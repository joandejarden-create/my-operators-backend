    WELCOME TO Krystal® Cancún

    DISCOVER A NEW WAY TO ENJOY VACATIONS

    We are in Punta Cancún, just minutes away from shopping malls, restaurants and the best nightlife.

    Our Cancún hotel offers room-only, room with breakfast, or all-inclusive plans, where you’ll have access to our four national and international restaurants and three bars with a wide variety of tequilas, wines and liquors.

    Whether you’re traveling as a couple, family, or with friends, relax on a bali-style bed by the sea, enjoy our panoramic pool with your favorite cocktail, or take the little ones to Kamp Krystal® for outdoor activities so they can have fun too.

    Our location

    is unbeatable

    At the heart of the hotel, just a stone’s throw from the best night clubs, bars, restaurants and shopping malls, not to mention the convention center.

    ● Coco bongo and other entertainment areas are just 2 minutes away on foot
    ● Plaza Caracol and Plaza de artesanías Coral Negro is just 5 minutes away on foot.
    ● Cancun Convention Center is 2 mins away on foot.
    ● Tortuga Beach is 5 mins away by car.
    ● Cancun International Airport is 23 mins away by car.
    ● ADO Bus Station is 30 mins away by car.
