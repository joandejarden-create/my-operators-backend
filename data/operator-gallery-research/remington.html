Join The Team

Backed by nearly 60 years of hospitality management experience in the U.S. and a robust corporate infrastructure, Remington brings trusted leadership and operational excellence to the Caribbean and Latin America region. Remington has built a top-tier local leadership team across both corporate and local levels, ensuring hands-on, in-market support to owners and asset manager partners. By combining U.S. proven systems and local market expertise, Remington sets itself apart - delivering exceptional guest experiences while driving strong financial returns for our owners.

 

Following the strategic decision to expand its management platform into the Caribbean and Latin America market in late 2022, Remington has achieved substantial progress in establishing a strong and growing presence throughout the region. In 2023, Remington established a regional headquarter office for the CALA division in Miami in order to enhance operational efficiency, foster cultural understanding, and improve market accessibility.

 

Currently, Remington's CALA portfolio includes 18 existing and development properties, or roughly 2,500 rooms, in markets including Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Mexico, Puerto Rico, Cayman Islands, Belize, Panama, and Peru. Shaping the Future of Hospitality in the Caribbean and Latin America​
