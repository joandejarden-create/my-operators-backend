Ocean Reef Resort

Shoreline, sunshine and endless fun

Completed in early 2025, a $15+ Million Dollar Renovation brings a rejuvenated vacation experience at Ocean Reef Resort. If you’re looking for a great oceanfront getaway in Myrtle Beach, look no further! Located on the oceanfront in the exclusive Dunes section of Myrtle Beach, Ocean Reef Resort offers first-class accommodations and amenities, making it the perfect choice for a fun family vacation, romantic couples retreat, or group or golf outing. Our fully furnished rooms and suites come in varying sizes and styles to fit your party and budget, and all feature private balconies overlooking the beautiful Atlantic Ocean. With clean, comfortable accommodations and well-appointed rooms, Ocean Reef Resort will make you feel right at home during your Myrtle Beach Resorts vacation.

Relax With One Of Our

Really Awesome Amenities

Our accommodations may make you feel as comfortable as being at home, but no home has the same outstanding amenities as Ocean Reef Resort. Offering indoor and outdoor pools, hot tubs, a lazy river, a waterpark featuring the Lagoon Launcher water slide and a kiddie aquatic playground, guests can play all day without leaving the property.
