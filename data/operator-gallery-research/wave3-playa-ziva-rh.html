The all-inclusive Hyatt Ziva Rose Hall in Montego Bay, Jamaica, offers recreation and relaxation for the entire family. Nestled along the shores of the sparkling Caribbean Sea, blue skies and the scenic mountains of Jamaica serve as the perfect luxury vacation backdrop.

Accommodations range from elegant, intimate rooms for two to large suites with ample room for families and all include private balconies with gorgeous views. Exclusive swim-up suites provide direct access to a semi-private pool from your terrace and the resort features two other sparkling pools as well. Younger guests will love the KidZ Club, while adults will enjoy a day at the spa or a round of golf at the White Witch or Cinnamon Hill courses.

MONTEGO BAY, JAMAICA

On the northwest coast of Jamaica under the misty peak of lush mountains is the vibrant and welcoming Montego Bay. Piercing the turquoise sea with its jagged coast and white sand beaches, this Caribbean paradise comes to life through its rich port history, reggae beats, and signature Jerk cuisine. From the top of the world-famous Dunn’s River Falls to the bottom of its coral-laced, crystal clear waters, you can feel island life pumping through each river, busy street, and sunset. Explore dense jungle and mountain terrain. Tour grand manses and colonial plantations. Sink your toes into warm sand. Unlimited water sports, duty-free shopping, world-class golfing, and nightlife unlike anything you’ve yet experienced—you’ll be hard-pressed to find a dull moment in Montego Bay.
