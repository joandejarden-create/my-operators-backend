DEALALITY WEBHOUND TEST 3 — GOVERNMENT/PUBLIC-RECORD EARLY HOTEL OPPORTUNITY DISCOVERY (LOS CABOS / EAST CAPE)

Budget: exactly $5. Success = ~5–8 strong Class 1 Pre-Decision Project candidates (prefer 3–5 genuinely credible over 8 weak). Do NOT expand into the rest of Mexico to increase row count. Do NOT research Oleum, Proyecto Venado, Residencial Punta Venado, or Riviera Maya / Quintana Roo targets from Tests 1–2.

CORE QUESTION
Can Webhound repeatedly identify credible hotel owners and projects BEFORE a material hotel strategy, brand, or operator decision becomes publicly committed — using government records, planning documents, environmental filings, public registrations, and related early-stage evidence — in Los Cabos / East Cape (Baja California Sur)?

This is NOT: a hotel-announcement search; new-opening database; generic developer list; search for already-signed brands; broad owner-enrichment; contact-only exercise; or repetition of QROO Test 1–2 research.

GEOGRAPHY (STRICT)
Los Cabos and East Cape, Baja California Sur, Mexico only:
Cabo San Lucas; San José del Cabo; Tourist Corridor; East Cape; La Ribera; Cabo Pulmo area; Santiago; Los Barriles; relevant nearby coastal or mixed-use development zones.
Do not expand beyond this geography merely for more rows.

PRIMARY ROW GRAIN
ONE ROW = ONE OWNER/DEVELOPER × ONE SITE/ASSET/PROJECT × ONE DISTINCT POTENTIAL DEALALITY DECISION.
Do not create generic company rows when a specific project/site can be identified.

TARGET MIX
- Aim Class 1 Pre-Decision Project candidates.
- Class 2 Pre-Project Owner Signals only when directly connected to BCS hospitality investment/development.
- Class 3 only if it reveals a separate still-early opportunity at the same owner; otherwise exclude.

DISCOVERY STAGES TO PRIORITIZE
Site/land identified; public application/filing; early planning; environmental review; entitlement/permitting; feasibility/concept; design development; hotel strategy formation; brand/operator exploration.
AVOID relying on: signed franchise/HMA; publicly confirmed brand/operator; groundbreaking with full hotel team announced; near opening; operating — unless another material hotel decision remains credibly unresolved.

GOVERNMENT / PUBLIC-RECORD FIRST (central mechanism)
Federal: SEMARNAT MIA / Gaceta Ecológica / environmental authorizations; public consultation; CONANP; coastal/federal-zone / ZOFEMAT where relevant; federal concessions; tourism-related federal records; official gazettes.
State BCS: environmental/development authorities; urban-development; planning instruments; official bulletin; tourism-development; water/infrastructure where material; investment/development-agency records.
Municipal (Los Cabos and relevant municipalities): land-use/zoning/rezoning; development/construction/demolition/change-of-use; master plans; subdivision; planning commission/council agendas/minutes; urban-development approvals; condominium/mixed-use filings; public consultation; permits/licenses; project descriptions.
Corporate/property (if legitimately public): SPV/commerce/corporate registry; registered representatives; landholder/property-owner; trust/fideicomiso; beneficial/controlling ownership when disclosed.
If a registry cannot be accessed, state that explicitly — do not treat absence of a result as proof no filing exists.

TRIANGULATION COMBINATIONS TO PRIORITIZE
LAND/SITE + PUBLIC FILING/APPLICATION + HOTEL/TOURISM/MIXED-USE/RESIDENTIAL COMPONENT + IDENTIFIABLE OWNER/APPLICANT/DEVELOPER + NO PUBLIC EVIDENCE OF FINAL BRAND/OPERATOR COMMITMENT.
Also valuable: new SPV + coastal site + environmental review; mixed-use master plan + hotel component + no public flag; branded-residence concept + unresolved hotel brand structure; environmental authorization + hotel keys + early design; land-use approval + hospitality intent + non-hotel developer; recently acquired site + development filing + hotel use; independent asset + redevelopment application; renovation/change-of-use before public repositioning; infrastructure application corroborating larger tourism project.

PRIMARY-SOURCE RULE
Prefer underlying filing/government record over press about it.
For each candidate attempt primary access and capture: issuing authority; filing/application number; filing date; status; applicant/promovente; project description; location; hotel keys/rooms if disclosed; residential/mixed-use components; named SPV/developer/owner/architect/consultant.
If primary not accessed: Primary Source Accessed=No; authority expected; source/database attempted; what prevented access; secondary relied on; confidence limitation.
Do NOT silently replace primary evidence with a newspaper article.

ENTITY / OWNERSHIP
Resolve: filing applicant; exact legal entity; project SPV; property owner if public; developer; parent; ultimate controlling owner; JV/equity partners; investment vehicle; trust/fideicomiso; ownership % ONLY if explicitly disclosed else "Not publicly disclosed".
Never treat promovente as owner without evidence. Never infer %.
Classify roles accurately: Property Owner; Project SPV; Applicant/Promovente; Developer; Development Partner; Equity Owner; JV Partner; Parent Company; Ultimate/Controlling Owner; Fund/Investment Vehicle; Trust/Fideicomiso; Asset Manager; Operator; Brand/Franchisor; Lender; Advisor; Unknown.

PRE-DECISION + TOO-LATE (mandatory for every Class 1)
Separate Confirmed Facts vs Research Inferences.
Fill why_may_still_be_open AND evidence_may_be_too_late.
Do NOT treat "no announcement found" as proof the decision remains open. Use: "No public evidence of a completed brand decision was identified in the reviewed sources as of [date]."
Actively search for brand signing, franchise/HMA, operator appointment, brand/operator pipeline pages, owner pipeline, project website, residence marketing under a flag, brand-referenced renderings, financing with named flag, groundbreaking/construction updates, opening timeline, trade press, investor decks, LinkedIn announcements. Downgrade if credible later-stage evidence found.
