Brand AI Visibility — How to Read

Audience: Brand development, brand strategy, and commercial leadership Product surface: Brand AI Visibility Last updated: 2026-08-17

Use this guide when reviewing Brand AI Visibility for the first time, or when sharing results with internal stakeholders.

1. What this product shows

Brand AI Visibility measures how your hotel brands appear when hotel owners, developers, and investors ask AI platforms commercially relevant questions about development, conversion, affiliation, and positioning.

It answers:

  • How often does my brand appear?
  • On which questions is it missing?
  • How do providers differ?
  • Which peers appear instead?
  • Which sources are cited alongside the answers?
  • How does English compare with Spanish (when both are monitored)?
  • Are key public brand / development pages reachable?

It does not tell you that Dealality recommends a brand, that AI “prefers” a brand, or that a cited website caused an answer.

2. Start here: filters and views

Every number on the page is scoped by the filters at the top.

FilterWhat it controls
GeographyMarket region for the monitored question set (for example, CALA)
ProviderWhich AI platform’s answers are measured, or All Providers (derived view)
LanguageMonitoring language (for example, English or Spanish), when more than one is available
BrandDetailed View only — which entitled brand you are inspecting

Two views

  1. Executive Summary — portfolio position, brand comparison, and the main takeaways.
  2. Detailed View — one brand at a time: provider differences, question gaps, peers, sources, and discoverability.

Tip: Read Executive Summary first. Open Detailed View when you want the exact question, provider, or evidence behind a finding.

3. Freshness and monitoring mode

Look at the freshness strip before interpreting any KPI.

LabelMeaning
Latest monitored resultsYou are looking at the most recent completed monitoring window
Providers completedHow many of the expected providers finished for this window (for example, 4 of 4)
Partial monitoringSome providers finished; others did not — do not treat unfinished providers as 0%
Not MonitoredNo completed comparable data for this filter combination

Today’s pilot monitoring is manually governed: results are updated when a monitoring run is completed. This is not a real-time or continuous feed.

4. Core metrics

AI Presence

What it is Share of monitored owner questions where the brand was observed in the AI answer.

How to read

  • Higher % = the brand appeared in more monitored answers.
  • 0% = monitored, but not observed.
  • — / Not Monitored = no comparable data yet — not a zero score.

Why it matters It shows whether owners asking AI about development and positioning are seeing your brand at all.

What it is not Not a recommendation score, preference score, or brand-health index.

Portfolio AI Presence

What it is Share of monitored owner questions where at least one of your linked brands appeared.

How to read This is portfolio coverage of the question set, not an average of brand Presence rates.

Why it matters It answers: “When owners ask these questions, does *any* brand from our set show up?”

Competitive Position

What it is This brand’s rank by AI Presence among a fixed peer set in the selected geography and provider.

How to read

  • #1 of 15 = highest Presence among peers in that filter set.
  • A higher number means more peers outrank this brand on Presence.
  • Rank uses Observed Presence only.

Why it matters It shows whether you are leading, keeping pace, or trailing peers on the questions owners ask AI.

What it is not Not a composite GEO score. Not available as a peer rank under All Providers (use a specific provider for rank).

Questions Missing

What it is Monitored questions where this brand did not appear in the AI answer.

How to read Usually shown as % (count) — for example, 8.3% (1) means 1 of 12 questions. Open Detailed View → Questions Missing Watchlist for the exact questions.

Why it matters Missing questions are concrete gaps: places where owners asked AI and your brand was absent — sometimes while peers still appeared.

What it is not Missing does not automatically mean poor product fit, weak brand quality, or that you should change content tomorrow without review.

Δ vs prior run

What it is Change in AI Presence versus the previous comparable monitoring run (same geography, provider, language, and compatible methodology).

How to read Shown in percentage points (pp). Positive = Presence improved; negative = Presence declined. If periods are not comparable, you will see Not Comparable or Insufficient History — not a fabricated 0.

Why it matters It tells you whether visibility is moving, not only where you stand today.

Top Decision Territory

What it is The owner-intent question family where this brand appeared most often.

How to read A label such as “Conversion” or “Collection / Soft Brand.” It is the strongest family by Presence for that brand — not a weighted priority score across all families.

Why it matters It shows which owner decision themes already surface your brand, and contrasts with families where you are missing.

5. Providers and All Providers

Brand AI Visibility can show results for individual providers (for example, OpenAI, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude) and for All Providers.

Single provider

Use a single provider when you want a clean story for one assistant owners may use, including Competitive Position rank.

All Providers

What it is A derived comparison across completed providers for the same geography and language.

How to read

  • Presence is typically an average of provider Presence rates.
  • Questions Missing (All Providers) highlights questions where the brand was missing across comparable providers.
  • Provider disagreement is preserved when some providers include the brand and others do not.

What it is not Not a fifth combined AI model. Not a single “consensus score.” Not a substitute for reading provider differences.

Practical tip Start with one provider for the opening narrative. Switch to All Providers when you want to show disagreement or “missing everywhere.”

6. Detailed View sections

Brand Overview / Brand Detail

Topline Presence, Competitive Position, Questions Missing, and change versus the prior comparable run for the selected brand and filters.

Provider Presence

Per-provider Observed Presence. Not Monitored and provider errors never display as 0%.

Owner-Intent Coverage

Presence broken down by owner-decision question family (prompt family). Use this to see whether gaps cluster in themes such as Branded Residences, Conversion, or Soft-Brand Affiliation.

Questions Missing Watchlist

The exact monitored questions where the brand was absent. Where available, also review Peers Appearing Where We Are Not.

Competitive / Peer Analysis

How the selected brand ranks among the governed peer set under the selected provider.

Citation & Source Intelligence

MetricHow to read
Citation RateShare of responses that included at least one citation
Owned Source CoverageShare of responses that cited at least one configured brand / parent / development domain
Citation FrequencyHow often a domain appeared across responses
Source MixOwned vs external citation patterns

Important: Cited sources are associated with answers. They are not proven causes of Presence or Missing, and they are not ranking-power scores.

Public Discoverability

A separate diagnostic from AI Presence.

It checks whether governed public URLs (brand, development, residences, and related pages) are configured and reachable, and may flag owner-intent content gaps.

State exampleMeaning
BASELINE_MEASUREDA governed public baseline check has been completed
CHECK_NOT_RUNURLs may exist, but the discoverability check has not been run yet

Discoverability is not an SEO score and not a Presence score.

Language Comparison

When English and Spanish (or other languages) are both monitored for the same geography and comparable cohort, Presence and Missing can be compared side by side.

Language differences are observed. They are not proof of market preference.

7. Evidence drawer

When you open evidence for a Presence or Missing fact, you should see the monitored question, the provider response for that run, and associated citations where available.

Use evidence to answer:

  • What exactly did the model say?
  • Which brands were named?
  • Which URLs were cited?

Do not treat a single response as permanent truth. Outputs can vary by run and provider.

8. How to read a finding well

A strong reading usually follows this path:

  1. Confirm filters (geography, provider, language).
  2. Note freshness (latest monitored date; providers completed).
  3. Read the portfolio or brand KPI.
  4. Open the question or family behind the gap.
  5. Check whether peers appear where your brand does not.
  6. Compare providers if disagreement matters.
  7. Open evidence before deciding what to do internally.
  8. Keep Discoverability and citations as supporting context — not as proof of cause.

9. Common misreads to avoid

MisreadBetter reading
“91.7% means AI recommends us.”It means the brand was observed in 91.7% of monitored answers.
“Missing means our brand is weak.”It means the brand was absent from that answer under these filters.
“All Providers is the true score.”It is a derived comparison, not a combined model.
“Hilton was cited, so Hilton caused our miss.”Citations are associated, not causal.
“Discoverability is low, so Presence is low.”They are separate diagnostics.
“Not Monitored means 0%.”Not Monitored means no comparable data yet.
“This is live.”Results reflect the latest completed monitoring window.

10. Suggested first-read sequence (10 minutes)

  1. Set Geography, Provider, and Language.
  2. Read Portfolio Snapshot and Your Brands.
  3. Note strongest and weakest brands.
  4. Open the strongest brand in Detailed View.
  5. Scan Provider Presence and Owner-Intent Coverage.
  6. Open Questions Missing and one evidence record.
  7. Skim Citation & Source Intelligence.
  8. Check Public Discoverability as a separate diagnostic.
  9. If relevant, toggle Language or All Providers and compare.

12. Reminder for sharing internally

When you circulate Brand AI Visibility:

  • State the filters and monitoring date.
  • Separate observation from recommended action.
  • Prefer one clear finding with evidence over many disconnected percentages.
  • Ask: “What internal question does this raise?” — not only “Is the number high or low?”
