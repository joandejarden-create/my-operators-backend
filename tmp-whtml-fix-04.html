Your Deal Process. Just Better.

Dealality seeks to eliminate or shorten up to 50% of the time and effort in early-to-mid stages—deal sourcing, matching, and early negotiations. Brands and operators focus only on real opportunities. Owners get faster, more competitive offers. Our goals:

Target: 80%
Less first-stage deal analysis — Owners submit pre-qualified info; brands only pursue aligned assets
Target: 40%
Reduced feasibility studies — Better data upfront; less speculative work wasted
Target: 50%
Less architectural analysis — Pre-vetted for brand fit; full PIP site visits only for serious brands
Target: 30%
Faster LOI drafting — Offers presented upfront; key terms compared side-by-side before legal
